Oslo, Norway, 25 November 2026 - MPC Container Ships ASA ("MPCC" or the
"Company", Oslo Børs Ticker: MPCC), today announced its results for the third
quarter of 2026, reporting solid operational and financial performance in a
firm charter market, with substantial coverage through 2027 and into 2028 and
2029.

Highlights Q3 2026:

* Charter backlog of USD 2.2 billion with 100% contract coverage for 2026 and
  high coverage for 2027 (90%), 2028 (66%) as well as 2029 (42%)
* Quarterly recurring dividend of USD 0.04 per share
* Continued robust operations with high fleet utilization of 97.8% (Q3 2025:
  97.6%) and adj. average TCE of USD 25,333 per day (Q3 2025: USD 26,523)
* Operating revenues of USD 113.1 million (Q3 2025: USD 125.9 million) and
  EBITDA of USD 69.9 million (Q3 2025: USD 83.5 million). EBITDA adjusted for
  non-recurring items was USD 60.8 million (Q3 2025: USD 74.9 million), and
  adjusted profit was USD 36.1 million (Q3 2025: USD 45.0 million)
* Completed an oversubscribed private placement in July, raising gross
  proceeds of approximately USD 108 million
* The balance sheet remains solid, with 28 debt-free vessels and a leverage
  ratio of 26.7%
* Took delivery of the methanol dual-fuel newbuilding DP World Southampton in
  August, delivered the 2007-built AS Angelina to its new owners and sold the
  2008-built AS Sicilia subject to successful handover.
* As at 30 September 2026, the Group's fleet consisted of 64 vessels with an
  aggregate capacity of approximately 183,800 TEU, of which 16 newbuildings on
  order.
* Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed: Revenues of USD 460-470 million and
  EBITDA of USD 280-300 million

CEO Constantin Baack comments:
"Container demand has held up better than many expected this year, and charter
rates reached a four-year high in the quarter. That momentum is carrying into
2027: the fourth quarter has been very strong so far, our charter customers
are looking for ships, and forward vessel availability is at its lowest level
since 2005. With 90% of 2027 already covered and discussions under way on our
remaining open positions, MPCC is well positioned for the year ahead."

Co-CEO and CFO Moritz Fuhrmann added:
"Firm asset values and continued strong charter markets allowed us to continue
renewing the fleet in the quarter. We took delivery of one newbuilding, handed
over one of our less efficient vessels, while five modern vessels are
scheduled to join the fleet by early 2027. The proceeds from the private
placement in July have strengthened our balance sheet, and with moderate
leverage and 28 debt-free vessels we have the financial flexibility to keep
investing in the fleet, act on opportunities that arise and continue returning
capital to shareholders."

About MPC Container Ships:
MPC Container Ships ASA (ticker code "MPCC") is a leading container tonnage
provider focusing on small to mid-size container ships. Its main activity is
to own and operate a portfolio of container ships serving intra-regional trade
lanes on fixed-rate charters. The Company is registered and has its business
office in Oslo, Norway. For more information, please visit
www.mpc-container.com.
