Group highlights:

Third quarter

* EBIT strengthened to NOK 91 million (NOK 65 million) - strongest quarter
  since restart of NRC Group
* Revenue was NOK 1.8 billion, in line with last year despite significantly
  weaker SEK and EUR
* Maintain expectation of EBIT margin above 3.0% for 2026
* Finland continues to succeed, revenue grew 37%, EBIT was stable at NOK 31
  million as light rail contracts are still in early phase
* Sweden developing, improved EBIT of NOK 10 million (NOK 1 million),
  restarted civil business provides broader base for growth
* EBIT of NOK 15 million (NOK 5 million) in Norway, reflecting ageing and
  profit takeout of the current portfolio.
* Kept continues to improve, delivering EBIT margin of 5.3% compared to a loss
  last year.
* Gunnar Knutsen profitable with an EBIT of NOK 8 million while in transition
  after completing major multi-year contract
* Machine with an EBIT of NOK 27 million, develops well as new separate
  segment
* Tender pipeline at high level, uncertainty is timing - whether and when
  tenders are announced
* Order intake NOK 1,238 million (NOK 900 million)
* Order backlog NOK 7,880 million (NOK 8,535 million)
* Operating cash flow NOK -27 million (NOK -95 million)

Topics to be covered during the Capital Markets Update:

During the Capital Markets Update, NRC Group will present the progress made
during the first half of its 2024--2028 strategy period and outline the
priorities for the next phase, shifting from profitability before growth to
profitable growth.
 
The presentation will cover:

* Strategic progress and achievements since the 2024 Capital Markets Update
* Market outlook and growth opportunities
* Strategic priorities for profitable growth towards 2028
* Financial performance, updated financial ambitions and capital allocation
  priorities
