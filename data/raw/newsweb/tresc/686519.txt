Luxembourg  - 13 November  2026 - Subsea  7 S.A. (Oslo  Børs: SUBC,  ADR: SUBCY)
today  announced a sizeable(1) contract award  from Kosmos Energy Gulf of Mexico
Operations,  LLC, for the Tiberius Project(2) in the US Gulf. The development is
located in lease KC-964 at a water depth of approximately 2,200 metres.

The  scope of work covers the fabrication, transportation, and installation of a
14-kilometre steel catenary riser and wet-insulated flowline to the Lucius Spar,
along with the installation of an umbilical and subsea controls.

Project  management and engineering will begin in the coming months at Subsea7's
Houston, Texas, office. Offshore activities are expected to start in 2028.

Craig  Broussard, Senior Vice President  for Subsea7 US, said,  "We are proud to
work  with Kosmos on this deepwater  tieback. Drawing on our deepwater expertise
and  a  closely  aligned,  collaborative  approach,  we  have  developed a cost-
effective solution designed to support safe and efficient delivery."

(1) Subsea7 defines a sizeable contract as being between $50 million and $150
million.
(2) Kosmos 33.34%, Occidental 33.33%, Navitas Petroleum US, LLC 33.33%
