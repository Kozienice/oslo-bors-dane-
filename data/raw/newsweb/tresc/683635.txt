Oslo, 6 October 2026: Reference is made to the stock exchange announcement
published by BlueNord ASA ("BlueNord" or the "Company") on 6 May 2026, regarding
the successful placement of a new 5-year senior unsecured bond issue of USD 400
million with ISIN NO0013750828 ("BNOR18").

In connection with the contemplated listing of the BNOR18 Bonds on Euronext Oslo
Børs, the Company has prepared a listing prospectus which was approved by the
Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway on 6 October 2026 (the "Prospectus").

The Prospectus shall be available on the Company's web
page: https://www.bluenord.com/investors/credit-information/

The Company expects to submit an application to Euronext Oslo Børs for the
listing of the BNOR18 bonds shortly.

Admission to listing is subject to approval by Euronext Oslo Børs.


This information is subject to disclosure requirements pursuant to section 5-12
of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.

About BlueNord ASA
BlueNord is a strategically important European oil and gas company that

which support the energy transition. The Company has a 36.8 percent interest in
the Danish Underground Consortium that is responsible for oil and gas production
in the Danish North Sea. BlueNord is listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange and
trades under the ticker "BNOR". For further information, please visit:
