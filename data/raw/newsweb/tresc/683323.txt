Oslo, 30 September 2026. Reference is made to the offtake agreement dated 12
January 2023 (as amended from time to time) (the "Offtake Agreement") between
Barton Mines Company, LLC ("Barton") and Engebø Rutile and Garnet AS ("ERG"), a
wholly owned subsidiary of Nordic Mining ASA (the "Company").

Barton has today terminated the Offtake Agreement. The Company is of the view
that the termination is without merit and will take such steps as it deems
necessary to protect the interests of the Company and ERG. The Company expects
to remain in dialogue with Barton going forward with a view to negotiate and
agree a potential new offtake arrangement.

The Company will keep the market informed of further developments as required by
applicable disclosure obligations.
