Hydro is presenting a revised strategy set to capitalize on opportunities in an
increasingly complex geopolitical and economic landscape. With a robust
operational foundation, a resilient financial position, and a well invested,
integrated value chain, Hydro has the assets and capabilities to succeed in a
changing world.

Key highlights

  * Strong operational performance: Norwegian smelter production increased by 6
    percent YTD, reaching record levels. The Slovalco restart and the Qatalum
    ramp up to around 70 percent of capacity, are supporting higher aluminium
    production throughout the global portfolio.
  * Improvement program ahead of schedule: On track to deliver NOK 3 billion in
    annual improvements in 2026, compared with the initial NOK 2 billion 2026
    target, and well on track towards NOK 6.5 billion by 2030. Strategic
    workforce adjustment concluded with NOK 1 billion in annual run-rate
    savings.
  * Maintaining capital discipline: 2026 capital expenditure is expected at
    approximately NOK 12.5 billion, NOK 1 billion below the previous NOK 13.5
    billion guidance. Hydro is expecting capital expenditures of NOK 13 billion
    in 2027, and approximately NOK 14 billion on average from 2028 to 2030.
  * Commercial success: New offtake agreements for low-carbon and recycled
    aluminium signed with leading brands Mercedes-Benz, CHANEL Fragrance and
    Beauty, and cable producer Nexans, highlighting continued demand for low-
    carbon aluminium across market segments. Hydro's participation in the
    Siemens Mobility led Train2Train project highlights the opportunity space
    for value creation from Hydro's recycling capabilities in large public
    infrastructure projects.

  * Hydro will discontinue the Strategic Integrated Hedge Program: A stronger
    Hydro enables a simpler approach to market risk. No new hedge positions
    planned under the Strategic Integrated Hedge Program and existing positions
    remaining in place until maturity. The change will improve earnings
    transparency to market fundamentals and reduce complexity in the company.

The revised strategy presented at Hydro Investor Day 2026 builds on operational
excellence, disciplined capital allocation and selective investments in high
value growth opportunities. By leveraging its integrated business model,
renewable energy advantage, and close customer relationships, Hydro aims to
strengthen its position as a leading and reliable supplier of low-carbon and
recycled aluminium solutions to key Western markets, translating competitive
advantages into profitable growth and long-term shareholder value.

Defining the future of aluminium

Geopolitical fragmentation, disruptions to global supply chains and energy
markets, and increasing regionalization are reshaping the global aluminium
industry. At the same time, structural demand from electrification, grids,
transport and digital infrastructure support long-term demand for aluminium.

"As one of few fully integrated aluminium companies in the Western hemisphere,
Hydro can offer customers in Europe and North America a resilient supply of
advanced low-carbon aluminium solutions. By strengthening operational
performance and maintaining disciplined capital allocation, we are well placed
to capture growth opportunities and reinforce our role as a reliable supplier of
a material the world depends on," says Eivind Kallevik, President and CEO of
Hydro.

Hydro's key markets, Europe and North America, remain structurally dependent on
aluminium imports. Hydro is well positioned to supply these markets with a
complete offering of primary metal and recycling capacity as well as extrusions
capabilities, supported by long-term access to captive renewable energy, and
bauxite and alumina supply from Brazil.

Operational improvements strengthening competitiveness

Hydro continues to accelerate its improvement agenda, expecting annual
improvements of approximately NOK 3 billion in 2026, well ahead of the NOK 2
billion target previously communicated.

The company remains on track towards its NOK 6.5 billion improvement ambition by
2030.

The program combines operational improvements, procurement, commercial
excellence, and digital and AI enabled initiatives across the portfolio. Digital
technology is increasingly supporting predictive maintenance, production
optimization, better raw material utilization, digital twins and automated
quality control.

Restructuring Extrusions to strengthen positions and improve earnings

Hydro continues to optimize its Extrusions footprint to strengthen long-term
profitability and competitiveness in challenging downstream markets.
Consolidation of production in Europe and North America towards fewer, larger
plants is expected to increase utilization rate by 10 percent points, at current
market demand. Also, a more integrated network enables greater cross regional
optimization and flexibility. These measures combined are expected to deliver
NOK 700 million in improvements when completed, driven by lower fixed costs,
improved competitiveness and a more resilient business.

Disciplined capital allocation and strong financial position

"Sharpening our approach to capital allocation gives us the flexibility to act
on profitable growth opportunities when and where they arise. We see significant
opportunities to shape new demand and new applications for aluminium in high
value segments, like data centers, copper substitution, electrification as well
as building and construction," says Kallevik.

Hydro continues to prioritize financial flexibility and disciplined capital
allocation. Adjusted EBITDA was NOK 32.3 billion for the twelve months ending in
the third quarter 2026, while free cash flow was NOK 5.9 billion. Hydro's five
year average adjusted RoaCE was 12.1 percent, above the company's target of 10
percent over the cycle.

Hydro expects 2026 capital expenditure of approximately NOK 12.5 billion. Growth
investments remain selective and are prioritized based on market attractiveness,
strategic fit, and risk adjusted returns.

Hydro's capital structure policy remains unchanged, with an adjusted net debt
target of around NOK 25 billion over the cycle. The dividend policy also remains
unchanged, targeting a minimum distribution of 50 percent of adjusted net income
as ordinary dividend over the cycle, with a floor of NOK 1.25 per share. Since
2022, total shareholder distributions including share buy back programs amount
to approximately NOK 47 billion.

A stronger approach to managing market risk

Reflecting a stronger balance sheet, portfolio and financial flexibility, Hydro
has decided to not enter new positions under the Strategic Integrated Hedge
Program.

Existing positions will remain in place until maturity. As these positions
expire, Hydro will have greater direct exposure to underlying market
developments, providing a simpler and more transparent representation of market
exposure. The decision does not change Hydro's broader approach to financial and
commercial risk management.

Renewable power supporting resilience and competitiveness

Competitive renewable power remains fundamental to Hydro's aluminium portfolio.
Hydro has 9.4 TWh of equity hydropower production in Norway. This supports
Hydro's Norwegian based aluminium operations, with a combined annual power
consumption of approximately 17.8 TWh.

Around 90 percent of Norwegian smelter power requirements are covered through
2035. The combination of captive hydropower, long-term sourcing, and flexible
renewable assets supports cost position, operational resilience, and Hydro's
low-carbon advantage.

Low-carbon aluminium and customer proximity

Hydro continues to see customer demand for low-carbon and recycled aluminium,
although commercial value of greener premiums is developing slower than
anticipated.

Expected 2026 sales volumes are approximately 81,000 tonnes for Hydro CIRCAL and
 Hydro Recycled, and 470,000 tonnes for Hydro REDUXA. Hydro has also signed
13,000 tonnes of Hydro CIRCAL volumes in the U.S. from 2027 onwards.

The combination of regional supply, low-carbon production, recycling
capabilities, alloy development, and downstream expertise allows Hydro to work
closely with customers on solutions addressing both decarbonization and security
of supply. Hydro will continue developing its low-carbon and circular portfolio,
while linking larger investments to market visibility and customer commitments.

Advancing the technology and decarbonization roadmap

Hydro advances towards net-zero by 2050, with disciplined investments reflecting
market conditions and commercial opportunities. The targets of a 30 percent
reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030, and net-zero emissions by 2050
remain.

An important milestone was reached in 2026 with the first aluminium produced
using Hydro's HalZero electrolysis technology. Hydro continues to develop
HalZero alongside carbon capture, renewable power, biomethane, hydrogen, plasma
and other technologies needed to address emissions from mine to metal.

At Alunorte, the transition from heavy fuel oil to natural gas, renewable
powered electric boilers and biomass initiatives represent important steps in
reducing the carbon footprint of alumina production.

Positioned to create long-term shareholder value

Hydro's integrated portfolio provides flexibility to direct capital towards the
strongest risk adjusted opportunities across Energy, Bauxite & Alumina,
Aluminium Metal, Recycling and Extrusions.

The company's priorities remain clear: Improve operational performance,
strengthen underperforming positions, preserve financial flexibility, and
selectively invest where Hydro's assets, technology, customer relationships, and
integrated value chain provide a differentiated right to win.
