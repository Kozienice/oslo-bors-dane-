Singapore, 18 November 2026

Reference is made to the announcement made by Hafnia Limited ("Hafnia" or the "Company", OSE ticker code: "HAFNI", NYSE ticker code: "HAFN") on 18 November 2026 announcing the Company's third quarter 2026 results and intention to pay a special interim dividend (the "Special Dividend"). Our preference is to pay the Special Dividend by distributing all 20,356,061 Class A shares of TORM plc ("TORM" and those shares, the "TORM Shares") held by Hafnia to its shareholders (a "Share Distribution").  The completion of a Share Distribution requires cooperation and coordination with third parties. If the Company is unable to secure the cooperation of necessary third parties and resolve certain technical matters necessary to timely process and effectuate a Share Distribution, the Company intends to instead apply its existing dividend policy and pay a cash dividend for the third quarter of 90% of net profit.

We remain strong believers in industry consolidation. However, our approaches to TORM have not been reciprocated, and we believe continued resistance and the passage of time would erode the benefits we had hoped to deliver for our shareholders. We will therefore pursue other growth and consolidation opportunities to deliver value to our shareholders. The investment in TORM has been financially successful, and after careful consideration of our options, the Board has determined that a Share Distribution would be best for the Company and its shareholders to exit this investment.

The Company expects to announce whether it will declare the Special Dividend by way of a Share Distribution or apply its existing dividend policy, along with the dividend record date, no later than 25 November 2026. If the Special Dividend is declared and paid through a Share Distribution, the Company expects that the Special Dividend will satisfy the Company's anticipated regular dividend requirements for both the third and fourth quarters of 2026.  If the Company instead pays a cash dividend in accordance with its existing dividend policy, the dividend for the third quarter will be 90% of net profit, and the Company will separately determine its fourth quarter dividend upon reviewing its fourth quarter results in accordance with its existing dividend policy.

About Hafnia Limited:

Hafnia is one of the world's leading tanker owners, transporting oil, oil products and chemicals for major national and international oil companies, chemical companies, as well as trading and utility companies.

As owners and operators of around 180 vessels, we offer a fully integrated shipping platform, including technical management, commercial and chartering services, pool management, and a large-scale bunker procurement desk. Hafnia has offices in Singapore, Copenhagen, Houston, and Dubai and currently employs over 4000 employees onshore and at sea.

Hafnia is part of the BW Group, an international shipping group involved in oil and gas transportation, floating gas infrastructure, environmental technologies, and deep-water production for over 80 years.
