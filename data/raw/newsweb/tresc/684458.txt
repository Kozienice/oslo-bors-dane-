Sandnes, Norway, 21 October 2026: Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR, "Vår Energi")
reports strong financial results in the third quarter 2026 and announces
increased shareholder returns, whilst continuing to build long-term value.

Higher production expected in the fourth quarter

  - Solid performance on operated assets with 91% production efficiency YTD
  - Main turnarounds completed in the second and third quarters
  - Higher production expected in the fourth quarter with new projects and wells
onstream

Strong financial position

  - Significant CFFO post tax of USD 1.5 billion in the quarter
  - Successful issuance of USD 2.5 billion senior notes
  - Record high available liquidity of USD 7.5 billion
  - Reduced net debt with leverage at 0.3x
  - Well-positioned to capture upside from higher commodity prices

Unlocking long-term value

  - BlueNord combination progressing towards completion around year-end
  - DNO swap and Pandion acquisition completed in the quarter
  - King Development start-up, adding high-value barrels
  - High Impact Exploration Collaboration established with Equinor and Aker BP

Increased dividend with extraordinary component

  - Increased third quarter dividend and fourth quarter dividend guidance of USD
400 million[1]
  - Long term dividend policy of 25-30% of CFFO after tax over the cycles

"We are pleased to report strong financial results in the third quarter of 2026,
which is supported by high realised oil and gas prices.

With strong cash flow from operations of USD 1.5 billion post tax in the
quarter, materially reduced net debt over the year to USD 3.1 billion and record
high available liquidity of USD 7.5 billion, the Company continues to build
resilience and flexibility.

With the BlueNord combination expected to complete around year end, we are
building the largest independent producer of oil and gas in Europe, with an
increased long term production target of around 450 kboepd.

We continue to strengthen our portfolio and long-term value creation on the
Norwegian Continental Shelf. The Pandion acquisition and DNO swap completed
during the quarter, while we are progressing a portfolio of around 50 new
projects. We are further improving

performance and our outlook by increasing oil and gas recovery, accelerating new
projects and delivering exploration success. Combining technology, strong
partnerships and integrated ways of working through our project factory approach
enables us to realise more value from our resource base, faster and for longer.

Underpinned by continued high prices and strong financial performance, the
Company is increasing shareholder distributions with an extraordinary component.
The Company will distribute USD 400 million for the third quarter of 2026 and is
also guiding USD 400 million for the fourth quarter of 2026[1]. Which means for
the full year we will pay USD 1.45 billion compared to USD 1.2 billion for
2025."

Nick Walker, the CEO of Vår Energi
