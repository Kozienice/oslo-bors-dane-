Thor Bærhaugen, primary insider and employee-elected deputy member of the Board of Directors of Kongsberg Gruppen ASA, has on 29 October 2026 purchased 600 shares in the company at a price of NOK 332.00 per share. Following the transaction, Bærhaugen owns 37,154 shares in Kongsberg Gruppen ASA.

Anne Christine Kaupang, a close associate of Thor Bærhaugen, has on the same day purchased 29 shares in Kongsberg Gruppen ASA at a price of NOK 331.00 per share. After the transaction, Kaupang owns 6,601 shares in the company.

This information is subject to the disclosure requirements pursuant to Article 19(3) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (the Market Abuse Regulation) and section 5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
