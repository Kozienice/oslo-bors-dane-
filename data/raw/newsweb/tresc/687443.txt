Sandnes, Norway, 25 November 2026: Deputy employee representative to the Board,
Trond Myklebust in Vår Energi has bought 6000 shares in the company at a price
of NOK 42.79 per share. Following the transaction, Trond Myklebust holds a total
of 10 328 shares in Vår Energi ASA.

Details of the trades are included in the attached notification of trading. This
information is subject to the disclosure requirements pursuant to MAR article 19
and Section 5-12 the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
