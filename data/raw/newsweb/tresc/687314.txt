Sandnes, Norway, 24 November 2026: Reference is made to the stock exchange
announcement made by Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) ("Vår Energi" or the "Company")
on 23 November 2026 regarding the agreement with ConocoPhillips to acquire its
Norwegian upstream business and related infrastructure interests in Norway and
the United Kingdom, including the Greater Ekofisk Area and the Teesside terminal
(the "Transaction").

Vår Energi is contemplating to carry out a private placement (the "Private
Placement") of approximately 249,000,000 new ordinary shares in the Company (the
"Offer Shares"), at a price per Offer Share (the "Offer Price") to be determined
through an accelerated bookbuilding process. Eni International B.V. ("Eni"), Vår
Energi's majority shareholder, has expressed its full support for the
Transaction and the Private Placement and will subscribe for and be allocated
for an amount equivalent to around USD 250 million in the Private Placement
subject to the Private Placement being fully subscribed.

The Private Placement is expected to increase the free float and have a positive
effect on Vår Energi's weight in selected domestic and international equity
indices.

The Company has retained Barclays Europe SE ("Barclays") and SB1 Markets AS
("SB1 Markets") as Joint Global Coordinators and Joint Bookrunners (the "Joint
Global Coordinators"), and Jefferies GmbH as Co-Global Coordinator (together
with the Joint Global Coordinators, the "Managers") in connection with the
Private Placement.

The net proceeds from the Private Placement will be used to reinforce the
Company's balance sheet in light of the Transaction, thereby strengthening its
financial flexibility to execute its previously communicated growth plans. The
Private Placement is not conditional upon completion of the Transaction.
Completion of the Transaction remains subject to customary conditions, including
relevant regulatory and governmental approvals, and is expected around the end
of first quarter 2027.

Details of the Private Placement

The Offer Price and the final number of Offer Shares to be issued in connection
with the Private Placement will be determined by the Company's Board of
Directors (the "Board") in agreement with the Joint Global Coordinators, based
on an accelerated bookbuilding process.

The bookbuilding period in the Private Placement will commence immediately
following the publication of this announcement and close before allocation (the
"Bookbuilding Period"). Notification of allocation is expected to take place on
or about 25 November 2026. The Company, in agreement with the Joint Global
Coordinators, may, however, at any time resolve to shorten or extend the
Bookbuilding Period, and may up until notification of allocation cancel the
Private Placement, at its sole discretion for any reason on short notice or
without notice. If the Bookbuilding Period is shortened or extended, the other
dates referred to herein may be changed accordingly. Neither the Company nor the
Managers will be liable for any losses incurred by applicants if the Private
Placement is cancelled, irrespective of the reason for such cancellation.

Notification of allocation is expected to take place on 25 November 2026. The
Offer Shares are expected to be tradable from 26 November 2026 (T+1) subject to
and following registration of the share capital increase pertaining to the
Private Placement with the Norwegian Register of Business Enterprises. Payment
and delivery of the Offer Shares is expected to take place on or about 27
November 2026 (T+2) on a delivery versus payment ("DVP") basis, to be
facilitated by a pre-funding arrangement regulated by the Placing Agreement (as
defined below). The Offer Shares will be delivered through Euronext Securities
Oslo (the "VPS").

The Company will announce the results of the Private Placement, including the
Offer Price and number of Offer Shares allocated in the Private Placement,
through an announcement expected to be published before opening of trade on 25
November 2026.

The Private Placement will be made pursuant to applicable exemptions from the
obligation to publish a prospectus and directed towards institutional and
professional investors (i) outside the United States in "offshore transactions"
as defined in, and in reliance on, Regulation S under the United States
Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the "US Securities Act") and (ii) in the
United States solely to "qualified institutional buyers" (QIBs) as defined in
Rule 144A under the US Securities Act pursuant to an exemption from or
transaction not subject to the registration requirements of the US Securities
Act. In any EEA Member State, this communication is only addressed to and is
only directed at qualified investors in that EEA Member State within the meaning
of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 as amended (together with any applicable
implementing measures in any EEA Member State) (the "Prospectus Regulation"). In
the United Kingdom, the Private Placement is only directed at relevant persons
(as defined below). The minimum order size and allocation in the Private
Placement will be the NOK equivalent of EUR 100,000, provided that the Company
may, at its sole discretion, offer and allocate an amount below EUR 100,000 to
the extent applicable exemptions from the prospectus requirements are available
as indicated above.

Allocation of Offer Shares in the Private Placement will be determined after the
expiry of the Bookbuilding Period by the Board, at its sole discretion,
following consultation with the Joint Global Coordinators. The Board will focus
on allocation criteria such as (but not limited to) pre-commitments, current
ownership in the Company and BlueNord ASA, timeliness of the application, price
leadership, relative order size, sector knowledge, perceived investor quality
and investment horizon, however Eni as the pre-committed investor will receive
full allocation. The Board and the Joint Global Coordinators reserve the right,
at their sole discretion, to take into account the creditworthiness of any
applicant. The Company may, at its sole discretion, set a maximum allocation to
any applicant as well as reject or reduce any application in whole or in part.
There is no guarantee that any potential investor will be allocated Offer
Shares.

The decision to launch the Private Placement is made under the authorisation
granted to the Board by the extraordinary general meeting on 17 August 2026 (the
"EGM"), pursuant to which the Board is authorised to increase the share capital
without pre-emption rights for existing shareholders. The completion of the
Private Placement by delivery of Offer Shares to investors is subject to (i)
resolution by the Board to issue and allocate the Offer Shares, (ii) the placing
agreement entered into between the Company and the Managers (the "Placing
Agreement") remaining in full force and effect, and (iii) the share capital
increase pertaining to the Offer Shares being registered with the Norwegian
Register of Business Enterprises and the Offer Shares being validly issued in
the VPS (together, the "Conditions"). The Private Placement may be cancelled if
the Conditions are not fulfilled.

To facilitate prompt registration of the share capital increase pertaining to
the issuance of the Offer Shares and DVP settlement, the Joint Global
Coordinators will pre-fund payment for the Offer Shares pursuant to the Placing
Agreement. The investors will remain fully liable for payment for the Offer
Shares allocated to them, and the Joint Global Coordinators reserve the right to
sell or assume ownership of Offer Shares not paid for when due.

The Company has agreed to a lock-up of 180 days following the settlement date of
the Private Placement, subject to customary exceptions and a right to issue
shares in connection with the announced merger with BlueNord ASA.

The Board has considered different transaction structures, including the
difference between a rights issue and a private placement, and considered the
structure of the contemplated capital raise in light of the equal treatment
obligations under the Norwegian Public Limited Liability Companies Act and the
Norwegian Securities Trading Act. By structuring the transaction as a private
placement, the Company will be in a position to raise capital in an efficient
manner and with significantly lower completion risks compared to a rights issue.
In addition, the Private Placement is subject to marketing through a publicly
announced bookbuilding process and a market-based offer price should therefore
be achieved. The Board also aims to widen and strengthen the Company's
shareholder base by completing the transaction as a private placement. On this
basis, and based on an assessment of the current equity markets, the Board has
considered the Private Placement to be in the common interest of the Company and
its shareholders. As a consequence of the private placement structure, the
shareholders' preferential rights to subscribe for the Offer Shares will be
deviated from in accordance with the authorization granted by the EGM.

The Company may, subject to completion of the Private Placement and certain
other conditions, propose to carry out a limited subsequent offering of new
shares (the "Subsequent Offering") at the Offer Price, on customary terms,
eligibility and restrictions.
