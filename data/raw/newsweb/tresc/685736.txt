Luxembourg - 5 November 2026 - Subsea 7 S.A. (Oslo Børs: SUBC, ADR: SUBCY) today
announced  an agreement for the sale, by  Seaway7, part of the Subsea7 Group, of
its  heavy  transportation  business(1)  to  HEA Energy. The transaction remains
subject  to closing  conditions, including  any applicable regulatory approvals,
with  completion expected in the first half 2027. The commercial terms including
cash consideration are confidential at this time.

The  transaction constitutes the permitted business divestment referenced in the
merger  agreement  with  Saipem  and  will  trigger  a  special dividend of EUR105
million,  as approved at the EGM on 25 September 2025. The special dividend will
be  paid  at  the  earlier  of  the  closing  of the transaction(2) and the date
immediately preceding the effective date of the proposed merger with Saipem.

 1. Includes six open deck semi-submersible heavy transportation vessels of
    which five are owned and one is a chartered from a third-party.
 2. Expected to require up to two weeks to process and distribute funds
