Espen Lühr, primary insider and employee-elected deputy member of the Board of Directors of Kongsberg Gruppen ASA, has on 13 November 2026 sold 30 shares in the company at a price of NOK 341.00 per share. Following the transaction, Lühr owns 129 shares in Kongsberg Gruppen ASA.

This information is subject to the disclosure requirements pursuant to Article 19(3) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (the Market Abuse Regulation) and section 5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
