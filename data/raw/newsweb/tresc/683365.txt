Oslo, 30 September 2026 - Nordic Mining announces that Finn Ivar Marum, CEO of
Nordic Mining, has notified the Board of Directors of his resignation, taking
effect from today, 30 September 2026. The Board has appointed Otto Søberg as
interim CEO.

Mr. Søberg is an experienced executive. He previously held the position as CEO
of Kværner Energy, Kværner Oil & Gas, Aker Yards, and Agility Group. More
recently he was the CEO of Export Credit Norway. Søberg is now a board
professional and adviser. He will join the Company on 7 October 2026. Until
then, Kjell Roland, Chair of the Board, will oversee the Company's executive
management.

Mr. Marum is resigning to take up an executive position outside the mining
sector. He will remain available as adviser to the Board and CEO for the
remainder of the year with a particular focus on permitting and government
affairs.

"Finn Ivar has led Nordic Mining through a demanding phase and built a robust
team with the expertise needed for the continued ramp-up at Engebø. On behalf of
the Board, I would like to thank him for his contribution. Our interim CEO, Otto
Søberg, brings extensive leadership experience and will ensure continuity and
momentum in the ramp-up", says Kjell Roland, Chair of the Board.
