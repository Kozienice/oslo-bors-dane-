20 November, 2026 - Aker Solutions has been awarded a limited notice to proceed
contract by Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro for the ACT project in Holyrood,
Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

The ACT Project is a key component of the province's strategy to provide
additional generating capacity to support the electrical grid during periods of
peak demand and emergency power requirements.

Aker Solutions' scope of work includes engineering, procurement, and
construction management (EPCm) services for a new 150 MW combustion turbine at
the existing Holyrood thermal generating plant site, approximately 31 kilometres
from St. John's. The project also includes generator step-up transformers, a
terminal station and fuel storage tanks.

"We are pleased to establish a new customer relationship with Newfoundland and
Labrador Hydro. This marks a significant milestone for our Life Cycle business
in Canada, expanding our presence beyond the traditional oil and gas sector. It
reflects our ambition to grow and diversify our Canadian operations while
leveraging our expertise across a broader range of energy infrastructure
projects," said Ine Dolve, Executive Vice President and Head of Life Cycle at
Aker Solutions.

The project will be led from Aker Solutions' office in St. John's, Newfoundland
and Labrador, with involvement from approximately 85 employees across Aker
Solutions and its subcontractors. Work on the project will commence immediately,
with final delivery scheduled for 2030.

The final contract award is subject to regulatory approval by the provincial
Public Utilities Board (PUB) and is expected in the first half of 2027. Pending
the approval by the PUB and final contract award, the contract value will be
confirmed, and the associated order intake will be recognized in the Life Cycle
segment.
