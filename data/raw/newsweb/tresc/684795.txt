With reference to the announcement from Nordic Mining ASA on 26.10.2026, Euronext Oslo Børs has decided to place the company in Recovery Box.

Recovery Box is a special compartment where Euronext Oslo Børs can place securities when the Issuer is subject to circumstances that make pricing of the securities particularly uncertain. Recovery Box is regulated in section 2.9.2 of Euronext Growth Oslo Rule Book II. The relevant security will remain in the Recovery Box until the circumstances for the allocation to the Recovery Box are no longer present.
