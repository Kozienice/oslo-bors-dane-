CEO Statement

This is my first quarterly letter as CEO of Hafnia. 

I have been part of building this company since its beginnings in 2010, and I took over a business with a clear strategy, a strong balance sheet, and an experienced team at sea and ashore. My role now is to build on these strong foundations and take the company further. I want to thank our former CEO Mikael Skov for 16 years of leadership, and our seafarers and onshore colleagues for their ongoing efforts, particularly through a demanding geopolitical period. 

More than six months after the conflict in the Persian Gulf began, the market is still far from normal. 
Crude tankers have taken most of the market uplift. Their exceptional earnings pulled more LR2 vessels into dirty trades, which reduces the effective capacity of the clean fleet. Diesel markets are tight, inventories are low, and refinery runs are set to rise from their seasonal low in October. Heading into the winter market, the product tanker market is very strong.  

Against this backdrop, Hafnia delivered a net profit of USD 170.8 million in Q3 2026, bringing our year-to-date profit to USD 628.3 million. Our fee-based business generated USD 6.3 million. Scheduled drydockings resulted in approximately 259 off-hire days in the quarter, and we expect approximately 99 off-hire days in Q4. 

Our average fleet TCE for Q3 was USD 32,708 per day. As of 13 November 2026, 74% of our Q4 earning days are covered at an average of USD 55,148 per day. That supports a strong end to the year. 

At the end of the third quarter, our net asset value (NAV) rose to approximately USD 5.2 billion, up USD 0.8 billion from Q2 2026. This is equivalent to USD 9.80 (~NOK 94.16) per share. Higher vessel values across all segments amid a strong market and a higher value for our investment in TORM have driven the increase. Our net Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio fell further to 11.9% at the end of the quarter, primarily reflecting strong operational cash flow generation and proceeds from our recent equity raise.

We remain strong believers in industry consolidation. However, our approaches to TORM have not been reciprocated, and we believe continued resistance and the passage of time would erode the benefits we had hoped to deliver for shareholders. We will therefore pursue other growth and consolidation opportunities to deliver value to our shareholders. In the coming quarters, we plan to exercise eight purchase options totaling approximately USD 235 million, adding the vessels to our owned fleet at prices well below their current market value. The investment in TORM has been financially successful, and after careful consideration of our options, the Board has determined that it would be best to exit our investment in TORM, preferably through a distribution to our shareholders of all our TORM shares as a special dividend.  

The value of such a distribution would be well in excess of the maximum dividend distribution contemplated by our dividend policy and would also satisfy our dividend obligations for both Q3 and Q4 2026. If successfully completed, this distribution would pass the value of our TORM shares directly to shareholders and let each shareholder decide whether to hold or sell.

Based on TORM's closing share price of USD 33.73 on November 17, 2026, the special dividend would have a total value of approximately USD 686.6 million, or USD 1.2826 per Hafnia share. Hafnia would distribute 1 TORM share for every 26.3 Hafnia shares held, equivalent to 0.0380 TORM shares per Hafnia share. Entitlements that do not add up to a whole TORM share would be paid equivalent in cash, currently at USD 1.2826 for each Hafnia share they relate to. The special dividend would replace our cash dividends for Q3 and Q4 2026. The ratio would be fixed in shares, not dollars, and there would be no adjustment if the TORM share price moves before payment. Upon completion, the distribution would reduce Hafnia's NAV by approximately USD 1.28 per share. For more information, please see our separate announcement regarding the Company's plans for the potential special dividend.

Our ability to make such a distribution depends on cooperation from third parties. If we are unable to obtain that cooperation by November 25, 2026, we intend to declare and pay a cash dividend for the quarter of 90% of net profit, in accordance with our dividend policy. We expect to announce the distribution, along with the dividend record date, no later than November 25, 2026.

After Q1 2027, dividends will follow our adjusted dividend policy, under which we will pay out 100% of net profits as dividends when our net LTV is below 10%, and 90% when our net LTV is 10% or higher but below 20%.

From a standing start in 2010, Hafnia has grown into one of the world's largest product tanker companies through disciplined capital allocation. While the geopolitical and economic outlook remains uncertain, our scale and experienced team give us a solid foundation for the period ahead.

- Søren Steenberg Jensen, CEO Hafnia
