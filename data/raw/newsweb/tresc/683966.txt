OSLO, Norway (13 October 2026) - TGS, a global provider of energy data and
intelligence, in joint venture with SLB, announces the Pelotas Sul Phase 2
multi-client 3D survey offshore southern Brazil. The survey will cover
approximately 11,200 square kilometers.

A Ramform Titan-class vessel is scheduled to commence data acquisition in
October 2026, with completion in Q2/Q3 2027.

Building on Pelotas Sul Phase 1, acquired earlier this year, the new survey will
further expand TGS' high-quality 3D seismic coverage in a frontier basin that
continues to attract industry interest. The Pelotas Basin is supported by
geological analogs along the conjugate margin and de-risked by discoveries in
Namibia. The basin offers a broad range of underexplored play concepts,
including turbidite systems, deep-water channels, and stratigraphic and
structural traps.

Pelotas Sul Phase 2 includes a balanced mix of held and open acreage. This
provides data coverage relevant to companies evaluating existing acreage, while
also creating opportunities linked to future licensing rounds and potential
farm-in activity.

Kristian Johansen, CEO of TGS, commented: "Pelotas Sul Phase 2 builds on the
strong foundation established by Phase 1 and further expands our coverage in a
basin that is attracting growing client interest. The project will provide
additional high-quality subsurface insight across both held and open acreage,
supporting our customers as they evaluate existing positions and future
exploration opportunities in the southern Pelotas Basin."
