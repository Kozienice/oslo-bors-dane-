OSLO, Norway (12 November 2026) - TGS, a leading global provider of energy data
and intelligence, is pleased to announce the award of an OBN contract offshore
Namibia. A deep-water crew is scheduled to mobilize in Q4, with the contract
expected to have a duration of approximately 120 days.
?
Kristian Johansen, CEO of TGS, commented, " We are very pleased to secure this
OBN contract offshore Namibia for a repeat customer, providing visibility for
one of our OBN crews into Q2 next year. There is an improving pipeline of OBN
opportunities for 2027, with activity predominantly centered around the South
Atlantic basins. We look forward to leveraging our acquisition capabilities to
deliver timely, high-quality data to support the client in making informed
decisions for further field development."
