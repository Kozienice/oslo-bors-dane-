The Board of Directors of Mowi ASA has resolved to offer eligible employees in
Norway, Scotland, Canada West, Poland, Germany and Italy the opportunity to
purchase Mowi shares at a discounted price.

A total of 1,209 employees subscribed for shares under the programme. To meet
these subscriptions, the company purchased 239,391 shares in the market on 25
November 2026 at an average price of NOK 226.59 per share.

Mowi's senior management and other primary insiders have increased their
shareholdings in the company through the programme. An overview of their share
purchases and resulting shareholdings is attached, together with the
corresponding transaction notifications.

This information is subject to the disclosure requirements under Article 19 of
the Market Abuse Regulation and section 5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading
Act.
