HARVESTED VOLUME OF SALMON AND TROUT

Total harvested volume of salmon and trout in Q3 2026 was 54,680 GWT, compared
with 59,100 GWT in Q3 2025.

Harvested volume by region for Q3 2026 (Q3 2025), measured in 1,000 GWT:

Lerøy Aurora: 19.0 (20.1)

Lerøy Midt: 18.8 (21.4)

Lerøy Sjøtroll: 16.9, of which 7.6 trout (17.6, of which 10.4 trout)
