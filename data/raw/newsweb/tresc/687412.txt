Veidekke has been awarded the contract to build a new main fire station for
Ålesund Municipality. The project will be delivered as a design and build
contract and is valued at approximately NOK 130 million, excluding VAT.

The new main fire station will provide improved working conditions for employees
while strengthening the municipality's ability to respond to fires, accidents
and other emergency situations. The facility will serve both Ålesund Fire and
Rescue Service and the Norwegian Civil Defence in Ålesund. A new access route
for emergency vehicles will also be constructed.
"We are very pleased to have been entrusted with building the new main fire
station in Ålesund. This is an important community project that will support
effective emergency preparedness and provide good working conditions for fire
and rescue personnel. We look forward to a successful collaboration with Ålesund
Municipality and the other stakeholders involved in the project," says Stig
Mork, District Manager of Veidekke Bygg Møre og Romsdal.
The new main fire station will be located at Lerstadveien 525. The project
combines new construction with the reuse of parts of the existing building
structure. The facility will have a total gross floor area of 4,140 square
metres. The operational section, including appliance bays and workshops, will be
constructed as a single-storey building, while the central section will comprise
two storeys arranged around an open atrium.
The building will include appliance bays for emergency vehicles, a wash bay and
workshop, technical infrastructure for operations and emergency preparedness,
office and administrative areas, changing facilities, staff rooms and support
functions for personnel. New outdoor areas designed to support operational
activities will also be established.
"This is one of the largest municipal construction projects in Ålesund in the
coming years. Once completed, the municipality will have a modern, robust and
future-oriented main fire station that will help safeguard the safety of
residents for decades to come," says Øystein Eltvik, Project Manager at Ålesund
kommunale eigedom KF.
The project includes stringent environmental requirements. Among other measures,
low-carbon concrete will be used, facilities for a high degree of waste sorting
will be provided, and sustainable material choices will be prioritised. Existing
buildings and structures will also be reused where appropriate.
Construction work is scheduled to begin in the second quarter of 2027, and the
new main fire station is expected to be completed in the first quarter of 2029.
The contract will be included in Veidekke's order backlog for the fourth quarter
of 2026.
