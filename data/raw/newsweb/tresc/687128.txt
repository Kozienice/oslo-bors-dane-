Sandnes, Norway, 23 November 2026: Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR, "Vår Energi", "the
Company") has entered into an agreement (the "Transaction") to acquire
ConocoPhillips' Norwegian upstream business and related infrastructure interests
in Norway and the United Kingdom, including the Greater Ekofisk Area and the
Teesside terminal. The agreed enterprise value is USD 4.425 billion, based on an
economic date of 1 January 2026, with completion expected around the end of
first quarter 2027.

The consideration payable at completion is estimated around USD 2.5 billion, net
of cash including cash flow generated by the acquired business from the economic
date to completion, as well as customary completion adjustments. Vår Energi is
also establishing a commodity hedging programme related to the Transaction
volumes for 2027 through Q1 2028. The Company has currently secured downside
protection for approximately 80% of the additional gas volumes at approximately
USD 110 per barrel of oil equivalent (boe) while retaining exposure to gas price
upside.

Following completion of the Transaction and the previously announced combination
with BlueNord, Vår Energi will further strengthen its position as the largest
independent E&P company in Europe and one of the top three global independent
offshore oil and gas producers[1]. The Transaction increases Vår Energi's long
-term production target to above 500 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day
("kboepd") into the mid-2030s.

Transaction highlights - Increased scale, operatorship, recovery potential and
value creation across a world-class asset portfolio:

  - Pro-forma 2026 production of around 550 kboepd, including BlueNord[2]
  - Raises the long-term production target to above 500 kboepd into the mid
-2030s

  - Increases total reserves and resources to approximately 2.9 billion boe[3]
  - Reserves and resource life of approximately 14 years[4 ]

  - Adds operatorship of the Greater Ekofisk Area and related export
infrastructure
  - Adding a highly competent team strengthening the capacity and competence of
the Vår Energi organisation
  - Expected to deliver USD 700 million to USD 1 billion of synergies[5] and
additional value creation potential, net present value post-tax
  - Increased free cash flow generation and dividend capacity resulting in
increased dividend guidance for the fourth quarter of 2026 to USD 450 million

  - Maintained leverage ratio NIBD/EBITDAX well below our over the cycles target
of below 1.3 and remaining committed to investment grade credit profile and a
strong balance sheet to pursue existing and future growth opportunities

Vår Energi will maintain a balanced production mix with around 65% oil and 35%
gas after the completion of the Transaction and the announced combination with
BlueNord, with continued low operating costs of approximately USD 13 per boe[6]
and top quartile emission intensity at approximately 10-11 kg CO? per boe[6].

Nick Walker, Chief Executive Officer of Vår Energi comments:

"This is another important step in Vår Energi's growth journey and further
strengthens our position on the Norwegian Continental Shelf (NCS). The
transaction adds significant scale, reserves and operatorship, while creating
the opportunity to unlock materially greater value from a high-quality asset
base with substantial remaining resources and development potential.

We see significant long-term potential on the NCS. Through the transaction we
will continue building on our strong track record of extending the lifetime of
fields, which might otherwise have remained underdeveloped.  This will
contribute to increasing competitiveness and longevity across the NCS, creating
further opportunities for industrial development and value creation for all
stakeholders and the Norwegian society.

The Greater Ekofisk Area is a world-class asset with significant remaining
resources, extensive infrastructure and decades of production ahead. It remains
one of the most important value creation hubs on the NCS. Supported by the
highly competent and experienced organisation, which will be fully integrated
into Vår Energi, we see substantial opportunities to increase recovery, mature
additional resources, and create value over time.

The transaction increases our long-term production target to above 500 kboepd
into the mid-2030s, reinforcing our position as the Europe's largest independent
E&P company and positioning Vår Energi among the world's leading offshore
independents, while further strengthening our role as a reliable and secure
supplier of energy to Europe."

The Transaction adds significant scale, increased operatorship and the
opportunity to materially increase value creation from a high-quality asset
base, including the Greater Ekofisk Area with substantial remaining resources
and development opportunities. Vår Energi sees significant opportunities to
increase recovery and unlock additional value through an increased operated
asset base, field developments and continued development of resources across the
portfolio. The Company has a proven track record of executing value enhancing
acquisitions and the Transaction is expected to generate between USD 700
millions and USD 1 billion of operational, commercial and financial synergies
across the portfolio, on a post-tax, net present value basis[5].

Vår Energi has strong available liquidity of USD 7.5 billion as at 30 September
2026. The Transaction is secured through existing available liquidity and cash
available at closing in the acquired company. Vår Energi is committed to
maintaining a strong balance sheet and investment grade credit rating, and the
Transaction is expected to have limited impact on the Company's leverage
position, with leverage remaining well below the target of below 1.3
NIBD/EBITDAX through the cycles. The Company intends to assess the opportunity,
subject to market conditions, to raise equity within the Board of Directors'
authorisation[7]. Vår Energi's largest owner, Eni S.p.A will remain a long-term
majority shareholder.

The Transaction is accretive on a per share basis on reserves, production,
earnings, free cash flow and dividend capacity[8]. This is reflected in
increased fourth quarter 2026 dividend guidance to USD 450 million.

Carlo Santopadre, Chief Financial Officer of Vår Energi adds:

"This transaction strengthens our position as the largest independent E&P
company in Europe. We are adding a significant portfolio of high-quality and
long life assets, providing material cash flow generation and further
strengthening our long term financial capacity and flexibility. With available

at the end of the third quarter 2026, we can fully fund this transaction through
existing liquidity while maintaining our leverage ratio significantly below our

We remain firmly committed to a strong balance sheet and our investment grade

2028. As of today we have secured downside protection for approximately 80% of
the additional gas volumes at approximately USD 110 per boe while retaining
exposure to gas price upside. The transaction has an immediate positive effect
on our free cash flow and dividend capacity, which is reflected in our increased
fourth quarter 2026 dividend guidance of USD 450 million."

High-quality asset portfolio with strong strategic fit and significant upside
potential

The acquired portfolio comprises interests in 13 producing fields across the
NCS, including the Greater Ekofisk Area, Troll, Heidrun, Alvheim, Oseberg,
Visund, Aasta Hansteen, Grane and Breidablikk, and related infrastructure. The
assets are low-cost and carry limited near-term abandonment liabilities, with a
balanced commodity mix and long reserve and resource life based on net 2P
reserves of 414 million barrels of oil equivalent (mmboe) and 2C resources of
101 mmboe as of year-end 2025. The portfolio complements Vår Energi's existing
asset base and increases its ownership in several core areas, with around 75%
overlap on a combined 2P reserves basis, reflecting the strong strategic fit and
existing knowledge of the assets.

The Transaction adds operatorship of the Greater Ekofisk Area, including
Ekofisk, Eldfisk, Embla, Tor and Tommeliten Alfa and Gamma fields, as well as
operatorship of the associated Norpipe pipeline and receiving Teesside
facilities in the United Kingdom.

Ekofisk was the first commercial discovery on the Norwegian Continental Shelf
(NCS), and the Greater Ekofisk Area represents one of the largest oil and gas
resource bases on the NCS. Initial oil in-place volumes are estimated at around
10 billion boe.  Building on a proven track record of creating value from assets
and applying new technology and solutions, Vår Energi sees significant
opportunities to increase recovery, mature additional resources and extend field
life, unlocking long-term value creation.

Torger Rød, Chief Operating Officer of Vår Energi, concludes:

"Through the transaction, we are adding a highly complementary portfolio of long
-life assets and a highly experienced and competent team. The increased scale

By applying our value-driven operating model and project factory approach, we
see significant potential to increase recovery, enhance performance and deliver

Together, this will strengthen our ability to grow the resource base, increase
production and extend the lifetime of our assets in a value-accretive way."

Completion

Completion of the Transaction is subject to customary conditions, including
relevant regulatory and governmental approvals. Completion is expected around
the end of the first quarter 2027.
