FRONTLINE PLC REPORTS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

Frontline plc (the "Company", "Frontline", "we," "us," or "our"), today reported
unaudited results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026:

Highlights

  * Profit of $641.4 million, or $2.88 per share for the third quarter of 2026
    and adjusted profit(1) of $530.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, or
    $2.38 per share.
  * Declared a cash dividend of $2.38 per share for the third quarter of 2026.
  * Reported revenues of $921.3 million for the third quarter of 2026.
  * Achieved average daily spot time charter equivalent earnings ("TCEs")(2) for
    VLCCs, Suezmax tankers and LR2/Aframax tankers in the third quarter of 2026
    of $148,500, $102,500 and $67,800 per day, respectively.
  * Sold two VLCCs built in 2017 for a total sales price of $270.0 million. The
    transactions generated net cash proceeds of $179.0 million, which were
    returned to the shareholders through the payment of a special one-time
    dividend of $0.80 per share in September 2026.
  * Entered into a three-year time charter-out agreement for one Suezmax tanker,
    built in 2016, at a rate of $80,000 per day, which commenced in October
    2026.
  * Entered into a new senior secured revolving reducing credit facility in an
    amount of up to $731.8 million in November 2026 to refinance the $1,286.0
    million secured revolving reducing credit facility, further reducing the
    Company's weighted average interest rate margin to 118 bps as of the date of
    this report.

Lars H. Barstad, Chief Executive Officer of Frontline Management AS, commented:

"The  third quarter of  2026 was defined by  the escalation in  the Middle East,
logistical challenges and time-consuming trading patterns. Utilization has since
reached  extraordinary levels, and  freight rates have  moved to all-time highs,
with  asset values following and the entire tanker complex benefiting. As we are
well into the fourth quarter of the year, our bookings so far are at the highest
levels we have ever recorded across all three of our segments. With the majority
of our spot days already fixed, Frontline is positioned to deliver its strongest
result  ever, both for the  fourth quarter and for  the full year 2026. The nine
months  that have passed  in 2026 is likely  to change the  oil trading patterns
considerably  in  the  future,  making  us  optimistic  for Frontline's earnings
capacity in the years to come."

Inger M. Klemp, Chief Financial Officer of Frontline Management AS, added:

"From June to November 2026 we reduced our financing costs through a combination
of  margin reductions on existing facilities for their remaining tenors and full
refinancing of selected facilities, contributing to a reduction in the Company's
weighted  average interest rate margin of 60 bps  from 178 bps at the end of the
first quarter of 2026 to 118 bps as of the date of this report.

We  believe  that  the  refinancing  of,  and  amendments  to, our existing debt
facilities  have been achieved on highly attractive terms, further strengthening
our  liquidity position  while reducing  our borrowing  costs and cash breakeven
rates.  We  continue  to  focus  on  maintaining our competitive cost structure,
breakeven  levels and solid balance sheet to  ensure that we are well positioned
to generate significant cash flow and create value for our shareholders."
