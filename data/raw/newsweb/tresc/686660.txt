November 17, 2026 - Aker Solutions has received a letter of intent (LOI) from
Allseas for the reception, dismantling and recycling of the topside from
Equinor's Statfjord A platform in the North Sea. The platform was originally
built at Stord in the 1970s and will now return to the yard to complete its life
cycle.

Statfjord A is one of the most significant installations in Norway's oil and gas
history. The topside was originally constructed at Stord in 1975 and prepared
for installation on the concrete gravity base structure in 1976. Since then, the
platform has been an important contributor to production from the Statfjord
field, one of the largest oil fields ever developed in the North Sea.

As the original builder of the topside, Aker Solutions has detailed knowledge of
the structure and extensive experience in maintenance, modification and life
-extension work carried out over several decades.

"Statfjord A has played an important role in Norway's energy history for almost
half a century. We are proud that Allseas and end-client Equinor have selected
Aker Solutions to handle the final phase of the topside's life cycle. The award
confirms the strong position we have built in decommissioning and recycling
through decades of efficient project execution," says Guro Rausand, Executive
Vice President for New Build at Aker Solutions.

The LOI covers disposal related preparatory work, reception, dismantling and
recycling of the approximately 50,000-tonne topside following offshore removal
by Allseas' heavy-lift vessel Pioneering Spirit.

Statfjord A is expected to arrive at Aker Solutions' decommissioning facility at
Stord between 2029 and 2031. Following arrival, the dismantling and recycling
campaign is expected to take approximately two years. At peak, the project is
expected to involve approximately 100 personnel at the Stord yard.

"Our decommissioning team has extensive experience in complex offshore
decommissioning projects. Combined with our specialized decommissioning facility
at Stord, this gives us a strong foundation for carrying out the work safely,
efficiently and with a high degree of material recovery," says Rausand.

Upon final contract award, Aker Solutions expects to book the above-mentioned
sizeable[1] contract as order intake in the fourth quarter of 2026 in the
Renewables and Field Development segment.

[1]Aker Solutions defines a 'sizeable' contract as being worth between NOK 0.5
billion and NOK 1.5 billion.
