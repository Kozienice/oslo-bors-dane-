(Bergen, 4 November 2026) Mowi achieved record-high revenues of EUR 1.73 billion
and a seasonally record-high operational EBIT of EUR 275 million in the third
quarter of 2026.

Mowi has never reported higher revenues or volumes than in the third quarter of
2026 and the company also achieved its best ever operating profit for a third
quarter. Mowi harvested a record 186,000 tonnes of salmon during the quarter, up
12% compared with the same period in 2025 while costs were down by EUR 51
million.

"It is very encouraging to see that we continue to deliver on key parameters
such as volume growth, price achievement and, not least, cost control, thereby
achieving competitive margins," said Mowi CEO Ivan Vindheim.

Volume growth is one of Mowi's strategic pillars, and estimated volumes for
2026 have now increased from 600,000 tonnes to 620,000 tonnes. This represents
growth of 11% compared with last year, versus expected industry growth of 4%.

"With the growth in volumes and revenues over recent years, Mowi is now not only
the world's largest salmon farmer but also the world's largest seafood company
by revenue. I am proud of the way we have developed as a company over time and
look forward this continuing," said Vindheim.

At the same time as Mowi continues to grow, strong cost control and more
efficient operations have made the company one of the most profitable businesses
in the aquaculture industry.

"It is positive to see our farming costs reduced by as much as EUR 51 million in
the third quarter this year compared with the same quarter last year, and by EUR
122 million year to date," said Vindheim.

Mowi Consumer Products, the Group's value-added processing division, delivered
another strong quarter with record-high revenues, supported by solid operations
and record volumes of 84,000 tonnes, an increase of 18% compared with the same
period last year.

Mowi's strategy of being fully integrated from fjord to fork has been a key
strength for the company. Mowi operates value-added processing facilities around
the world and works closely with many of the world's leading grocery retailers
and foodservice operators.

"Being fully integrated provides us, among other things, with greater
flexibility and makes it easier for us to support consumer demand. Underlying
demand for our products was very strong in the third quarter, and we expect this
positive trend to continue," said Vindheim.

Mowi Feed, the Group's feed production business, also once again delivered
record results and volumes sold, increasing by as much as 33% and 19%
respectively compared with the same quarter last year. This growth was driven by
favourable biological conditions in Mowi's Norwegian and Scottish farming
operations.

The Board of Directors of Mowi has decided to pay a dividend of NOK 2.30 per
share for the third quarter.

About Mowi ASA
Mowi is the world's largest seafood company and producer of farm-raised Atlantic
salmon, with an estimated 2026 harvest of 620,000 tonnes from seven farming
countries including Norway, Scotland, Ireland, Faroes, Iceland, Canada and
Chile.

Mowi is a fully integrated global seafood company, bringing salmon and other
seafood of supreme quality to consumers around the world, partly under its own
MOWI brand. Mowi has been widely recognised for sustainability throughout its
value chain.

With headquarters in Bergen, Norway, Mowi employs 11 500 people in 26 countries
worldwide, and is listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange. Total turnover in the last
four quarters was EUR 6.5 billion.

For more information, please visit mowi.com.
