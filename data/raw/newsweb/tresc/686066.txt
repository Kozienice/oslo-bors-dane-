Sentia ASA delivers a solid third quarter of 2026, with good activity, strong
project execution and several new contracts. Revenue increased by 12.9 per
cent compared with the same period last year.

Key figures Q3 2026
- Revenue: NOK 3,217 million (2,850), up 12.9%
- Profit before tax: NOK 191 million (189)
- Order intake: NOK 3,583 million (1,044)
- Order backlog: NOK 18,702 million (19,535)
- Net financial position: NOK 4,146 million (3,252)

-- We are delivering another quarter of high activity and good operations
across the group. Profitability is solid, and a strong order backlog gives us
a good starting point for 2027. At the same time, we know that good project
execution is not only about what we deliver, but how we deliver it. That
places high demands on leadership, collaboration and safety, and that is where
we will continue to stand out, says Jan Jahren, CEO of Sentia.

New projects in the quarter
During the quarter, HENT signed a contract with Nscale to expand the data
centre in the Narvik region with two new buildings totalling 75 MW. HENT will
thereby build three buildings with a combined capacity of 100 MW for Nscale.
HENT also signed an agreement with Calmar Gruppen for the refurbishment and
conversion of Klubbgata 3, located in Stavanger, into a hotel.

Sentia Sverige also started new projects in the quarter, including a new
preschool in Tegelviken in Kvicksund for Eskilstuna Kommunfastighet through
SSEA.
