Sandnes, 30 September 2026: Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR, "Vår Energi", "the
Company") has completed the acquisition of selected assets and licences from
Pandion Energy AS, as announced on 18 June 2026.

Through the acquisition, Vår Energi strengthens its position in the Gjøa and
Åsgard areas through a 10% interest in the producing Nova field (PL418), a 20%
interest in the Ofelia development (PL929), a 49% interest in the Sierra Solberg
discovery (PL263), and a portfolio of exploration licences in the Gjøa area.
Together with other recent transactions, this supports the Company's strategy of
optimising ownership around key operated hubs, extending infrastructure and hub
lifetime, supporting long-term production, value creation and cash flow
generation.
