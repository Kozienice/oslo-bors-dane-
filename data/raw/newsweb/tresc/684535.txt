The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency has appointed NRC Group Finland, a
company wholly owned by NRC Group ASA, to a contract for railway construction
works under the HERI2 project in Finland. The contract is part of the second
phase of the Helsinki--Riihimäki railway capacity improvement project in
Southern Finland.
 
The contract is valued at approximately EUR 59.2 million, and the works
include track, signalling, catenary, high voltage, substructure and
superstructure works, as well as bridges and road arrangements. The work will
commence immediately after signing of the contract and is scheduled for
completion in December 2029.
 
Closing date for submission of complaints is 14 days from today, and formal
contractual signing can take place after this period at the earliest.
