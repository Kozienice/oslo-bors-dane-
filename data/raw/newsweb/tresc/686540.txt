Widerøe will expand its fleet with two new Embraer E195-E2 aircraft, scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2028. The additions will bring the regional airline's jet fleet to five aircraft, alongside its existing 47 De Havilland Dash 8 turboprop aircraft.

The aircraft will be delivered directly from Embraer and leased from one of Widerøe's existing lessors. They will operate on Widerøe's commercial route network alongside its existing jet aircraft.

"These Embraer aircraft are modern, environmentally friendly and cost-efficient, and fit well into Widerøe's existing fleet. The two new, larger aircraft will strengthen our network between Bergen, Trondheim and Northern Norway and enable us to further develop our services across regional Norway. We are pleased that Norwegian supports the continued development of our fleet," said Tore Jenssen, CEO of Widerøe.

The new aircraft will deliver operational efficiencies and reduce emissions. The E195-E2 is the quietest and most environmentally friendly aircraft in its class, with up to 25 per cent lower fuel consumption and CO2 emissions per seat, as well as a noise reduction of around 60 per cent compared with previous-generation aircraft.

Widerøe became the world's first Embraer E2 operator in 2018, introducing three E190-E2 aircraft that have since served domestic and international routes.
