AF Gruppen ASA (AFG) has issued 685,225 shares as a private placement in line with the Company's share purchase program for employees, as informed in the public announcement as of 8 October 2026. The subscription price is NOK 150.26 per share. 

The share capital increase has increased the share capital in AFG ASA by NOK 34,261.25 to NOK 5,717,833.50. The total number of shares outstanding after this share capital increase is 114,356,670.
