Reference is made to the stock exchange announcement dated 19 May 2026
regarding the appointment of NRC Group Finland, a company wholly owned by NRC
Group ASA ("NRC"), and GRK Finland as construction parties for the first phase
of the Turku tramway project.
 
Turku Tramway Ltd and the City of Turku have approved a scope change under the
alliance agreement for the first phase. The scope change comprises
archaeological excavations and conservation works, and the total value is EUR
14 million, of which NRC's estimated share is EUR 6.5 million.

For further information, please contact: Åsgeir Nord, CFO of NRC Group, +
47 957 34 985.
