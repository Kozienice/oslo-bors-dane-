Oslo, 26 November 2026 - The Board of Directors of Polar Resources AS (the "Company" or "Polar Resources") (Euronext Growth Oslo: POLAR) has approved the launch of a share buy-back program that allows the Company to repurchase up to 10% its outstanding common shares.

The Company will enter into an agreement with a broker ("The Broker"), for the repurchase of the Company's common shares in open market transactions on the Euronext Growth Oslo. Under this agreement, The Broker will make its own trading decisions independently of, and uninfluenced by, Polar Resources, subject to instructions provided by Polar Resources in the agreement.

In order to comply with the European Market Abuse Regulation, the Company has provided the following required information: (i) under the repurchase program, as may be effected under The Broker agreement, the Company may repurchase up to 10% of its common shares during the period from 26 November 2026 until 30 June  2027, as approved at the Extraordinary General Meeting on 11 September 2026, and (ii) the purpose of the buy-back program is to reduce the number of common shares of the Company outstanding and to provide a return to Company shareholders. The number of shares acquired per day shall not exceed 25% of the average daily trading volume in the 20 trading days preceding the relevant purchase date. 

The Company cannot predict how many shares will be repurchased, if any, under the agreement with The Broker, or the timing of any repurchase or the price that will be paid for any shares repurchased under the agreement. The buy-back program will be completed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/1052. 

This information is subject of the disclosure requirements pursuant to section 5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act and article 5 of the European Market Abuse Regulation.
