Equinor (OSE:EQNR, NYSE:EQNR) delivered an adjusted operating income* of USD
12.35 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Equinor reported a net operating
income of USD 11.95 billion and a net income of USD 2.54 billion. Adjusted net
income* was USD 2.86 billion, leading to adjusted earnings per share* of USD
1.20.

Delivering on the strategy

  * Acquired gas-fired power plant in the US
  * Progressing Bay du Nord with new partner in October
  * Accessed attractive exploration acreage in Namibia, and established a
    strategic exploration collaboration on the NCS

Strong production, cash flow and financial results

  * Production growth of 2%
  * Increased 2026 production guiding to ~4%
  * Strong Marketing, Midstream and Processing results with record contribution
    from the Mongstad refinery
  * Cash flow from operations after taxes paid* of USD 8.1 billion

Capital distribution

  * Third quarter cash dividend of USD 0.39 per share
  * Fourth tranche of the share buy-back of up to USD 1,125 million
  * Share buy-back of up to USD 3 billion for 2026

Delivering more energy

Equinor delivered high production in the third quarter with a total equity
production of 2,166 mboe per day. This is a 2% increase compared to the same
quarter last year.

Production on the NCS remained high, and was stable compared to the third
quarter in 2025. New fields, such as Halten East and Verdande, and contribution
from new wells offset natural decline and unplanned losses from Johan Castberg
and Troll.

Production increased by 21% for the international oil and gas reporting segment
compared to the same quarter last year. Main drivers were the addition of
production from Adura in the UK and Bacalhau in Brazil, as well as lower
turnaround activity. This was partially offset by portfolio divestments and
natural decline.

The US production was lower than in the same quarter last year. Onshore
production remained high, while planned downtime and natural decline offshore
impacted overall production levels.

The renewable power generation increased by 26% due to contributions from Dogger
Bank and a new onshore asset in Brazil. The total power generation was 1.32 TWh.
Lower gas-to-power generation from Triton resulted in a decrease in total power
generation in the third quarter.

Growing cash flow with strong financial results

Equinor delivered an adjusted operating income* of USD 12.35 billion and
adjusted net income* was USD 2.86 billion in the third quarter. The results are
primarily impacted by higher liquid prices and European gas prices. In addition,
international liftings were lower than the company's share of production,
resulting in lower sales volumes.

The reported net operating income of USD 11.95 billion is up from USD 5.27
billion in the same quarter last year. The results were supported by higher
commodity prices and strong refining margins.

Equinor realised a European gas price of USD 20.0 per mmbtu and a liquids price
of USD 92.3 per bbl in the third quarter.

The Marketing, Midstream and Processing results were strong, primarily driven by
high refining margins and strong refining performance, as well as LNG trading.

Adjusted operating and administrative expenses* remained broadly stable compared
to the same quarter last year. Higher transportation costs and other variable
costs were offset by a lower impact of changes in decommissioning estimates for
a US offshore asset.

Elevated commodity prices and high production generated cash flows provided by
operating activities, before taxes paid and working capital items, of USD 14.68
billion.

In the quarter, Equinor paid the two first NCS tax instalments for 2026
totalling USD 6.2 billion.

Cash flow from operations after taxes paid* ended at USD 8.14 billion.

Organic capital expenditure* was USD 3.28 billion and total capital expenditures
were USD 3.82 billion.

The net debt to capital employed adjusted ratio* was 3.8% at the end of the
third quarter, compared to 10.4% last quarter.

Executing on strategy

On the NCS, Equinor, Aker BP and Vår Energi established a new strategic
exploration partnership to unlock greater value through closer collaboration. In
the quarter, Equinor also signed energy supply agreements with companies in
Poland, the Netherlands and Germany.

Equinor acquired bp's interest in Bay du Nord in Canada in the quarter. In
October, the company sold a 30% interest to Shell. The project maturation
continues towards an investment decision.

Equinor entered Namibia through an exploration licence interest and was involved
in new discoveries in Angola.

To further build its integrated power business, Equinor acquired an interest in
the gas-fired power plant Lackawanna in the US.

Equinor had exploration activity on 13 wells in the quarter. Six wells were
completed of which three were commercial. In addition a commercial discovery was
confirmed from one of the wells completed in the second quarter.

Capital distribution

The board of directors has decided a cash dividend of USD 0.39 per share for the
third quarter of 2026. This is in line with the communication on 4 February
2026, when results for the fourth quarter of 2025 were announced.

At the Capital Markets Day on 16 June this year, Equinor announced an intention
to increase the share buy-back programme for 2026 by USD 1.5 billion. This
brings the total expected programme for 2026 to up to USD 3 billion. The board
has decided to initiate a fourth and final tranche of the share buy-back
programme for 2026 of up to USD 1,125 million. The tranche will commence on 29
October 2026 and end no later than 1 February 2027.

The third tranche of the share buy-back programme for 2026 was completed on 22
October with a total value of USD 1,125 million.

All share buy-back amounts include shares to be redeemed by the Norwegian State.
