Øgreid Eiendom has decided to commence construction of Løvdahlsparken in
Stavanger, with Veidekke appointed as the design and build contractor for the
project. The contract is valued at approximately NOK 164 million, excluding VAT.

Løvdahlsparken will be developed in a central location in Stavanger and will
comprise 36 apartments, one commercial unit, 19 parking spaces, and associated
outdoor areas.
"We are very pleased to once again be entrusted with delivering a project for
Øgreid Eiendom. We know each other well from previous collaborations, which
provides a strong foundation as we move forward with an exciting residential
development in the heart of the city," says Bendik Aarstad, District Manager at
Veidekke Bygg Rogaland.
The project includes a variety of apartment types, several of which are designed
as family homes with qualities typically associated with detached houses. The
largest apartments measure 124 square metres and feature three bedrooms and two
private rooftop terraces.
The buildings will surround a landscaped green courtyard. A shared rooftop
terrace of 220 square metres is planned for residents, featuring recreational
and play areas. A new public park will also be established adjacent to the
development. In addition, the project will include a guest apartment and a
communal room.

The façades are planned in timber cladding with elements of brick. The
architecture draws inspiration from the area's existing wooden housing and aims
to combine a contemporary expression with the site's historic character. Einar
Jarmund & Co is the architect for the project.
"Løvdahlsparken is a project we have worked on for many years and for which we
have ambitious plans, and we are pleased that construction can now begin. More
than half of the apartments have already been sold. We are building homes for
people who want the space and qualities of a detached house while enjoying close
proximity to Vågen and the city centre. For those who have been waiting for the
project to become a reality, now is an excellent opportunity to secure one of
the remaining homes, particularly the larger apartments with balconies or
rooftop terraces and views of Vågen," says Erlend Aanestad, Managing Director of
Øgreid Eiendom.
Demolition works will begin in the first quarter of 2027, and the project is
scheduled for completion in the second quarter of 2029. The contract will be
included in Veidekke's order backlog for the fourth quarter of 2026.
More information about the project is available at: https://lovdahlsparken.no
