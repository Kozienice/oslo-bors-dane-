SalMar will present its results for the third quarter of 2026 on Tuesday 3
November 2026 at 08:00 CET. The presentation will be held at Hotel Continental
in Oslo.

CEO Frode Arntsen and CFO Ulrik Steinvik will be representing the company.

The presentation will also be available via a Norwegian-language webcast on
www.salmar.no from 08:00 CET. It will be possible to submit questions during the
presentation. A recorded English-language webcast will be available on
www.salmar.no from 10:00 CET.

A lunch presentation will be held at 12:00 CET following the morning
presentation. To attend, please register through SEB.

The results will be published at 06:30 CET on the company's website,
www.salmar.no, and on Oslo Stock Exchange' news service, www.newsweb.no.
