On 1 October 2026, the following primary insiders have purchased Bakkafrost
shares as part of the share savings plan for employees in 2026:

  · Regin Jacobsen, CEO, purchased 72 shares. After the transaction, he holds
directly and indirectly 4,658,717 shares.
  · Høgni Dahl Jakobsen, CFO, purchased 52 shares. After the transaction, he
holds directly and indirectly 82,876 shares.
  · Odd Eliasen, Managing Director, purchased 52 shares. After the transaction,
he holds 191,488 shares.
  · Annika Frederiksberg, Board Member, purchased 21 shares. After the
transaction, she holds directly and indirectly 17,780 shares.

The shares were purchased from P/F Bakkafrost. In addition to the shares sold to
the primary insiders, P/F Bakkafrost sold 2,462 shares to other employees as
part of the share savings plan. In total P/F Bakkafrost sold 2,659 shares, and
after the sale, the Bakkafrost Group holds 104,598 shares. The transaction was
based on the share price and currency rate at market close on 28 September 2026,
which were NOK 451.40 per share and corresponding to DKK 311.96 per share.

For further information about Bakkafrost's share savings plan, see
www.bakkafrost.com/en/investor/share-information/share-savings
-plan (http://www.bakkafrost.com/en/investor-relations/share-information/share
-savings-plan)
