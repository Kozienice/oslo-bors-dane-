Based on preliminary financial review, TGS expects multi-client investment to be
approximately USD 63 million in Q3 2026, compared to USD 85.9 million in Q3
2025.

Kristian Johansen, CEO of TGS, commented: "The normalized Ocean Bottom Node
(OBN) crew count in Q3 was 2.2, slightly above expectations. During the quarter,
TGS completed an OBN contract in the Gulf of America for a client where we have
a long-term capacity agreement and a node-on-a-rope contract in Europe. Despite
high streamer 3D vessel booking going into Q3, utilization came in lower than
expected due to operational challenges on a North Sea multi-client project that
was completed in September. Looking ahead, we expect streamer 3D vessel
utilization to remain high, with all our streamer 3D vessel capacity booked for
Q4."
