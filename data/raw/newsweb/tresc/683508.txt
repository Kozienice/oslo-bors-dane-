The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency has appointed NRC Group Finland, a
company wholly owned by NRC Group ASA, to a contract for bridge construction
and road arrangements in Siilinjärvi and Kuopio, Eastern Finland.
 
The contract is valued at approximately EUR 3.8 million, and the work will
commence immediately and is scheduled for completion in September 2027.
 
Closing date for submission of complaints is 14 days from today, and formal
contractual signing can take place after this period at the earliest.

For further information, please contact: Åsgeir Nord, CFO of NRC Group, +
47 957 34 985.
