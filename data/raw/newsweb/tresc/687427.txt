Sandnes, Norway, 25 November 2026: Reference is made to the stock exchange
announcement made by Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) (the "Company") on 24 November
2026 regarding the successful completion of a private placement (the "Private
Placement") of 249,000,000 new ordinary shares in the Company (the "Offer
Shares"), at an offer price of NOK 42 per Offer Share.

The share capital increase pertaining to the Private Placement has today been
registered with the Norwegian Register of Business Enterprises, following which
the registered share capital of the Company is NOK 439,265,000, divided into
2,745,406,246 ordinary shares and 4 B shares, each with a nominal value of NOK
0.16.

As a result of the registration, the Offer Shares are now listed and tradable.
For further information, see announcements on 24 November 2026.
