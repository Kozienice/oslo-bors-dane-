Lerøy Seafood Group ASA (Lerøy) has entered into an agreement to acquire 47.7%
of the shares in Sjøtroll Havbruk AS (Sjøtroll). Lerøy currently owns 50.7% of
the shares in Sjøtroll and will own 98.4% following the transaction. The
remaining minority shareholders may accede to the agreement on the same terms in
the period leading up to completion.

If the entire minority interest is acquired, corresponding to 49.3% of the
shares in Sjøtroll Havbruk AS, Lerøy will pay total consideration consisting of
16.5 million shares in Lerøy Seafood Group ASA and NOK 750 million. The market
value of the share consideration will vary with the Lerøy share price.

As at the end of October, Sjøtroll has:

  * 16,372 tonnes of commercial maximum allowed biomass (MAB) and 1,560 tonnes
    of MAB under broodstock licences
  * Fish in the sea corresponding to full utilisation of MAB
  * Roe and smolt capacity through its 50% ownership interests in Lerøy
    Kjærelva, Flatråker and Jakta which produce approximately 70% of all trout
    roe in Norway
  * Modern infrastructure at its farming sites

The MAB reduction expected in March 2027 is estimated at 827 tonnes. Closed
production capacity is scheduled to become operational in early 2027, thereby
releasing MAB. Sjøtroll's net interest-bearing debt, including its share of debt
in Lerøy Kjærelva, is approximately NOK 500 million.

"We are increasing our ownership interest in a fully integrated aquaculture
operation that we know well and that, following several challenging years, is
showing positive biological development in its production. I would also like to
take this opportunity to thank the family behind Hardhaus for more than 16 years
of cooperation," says Henning Beltestad, CEO of Lerøy.

"We are pleased to have reached an agreement with Lerøy. We believe the
transaction will result in a forward-looking ownership structure for Sjøtroll.
We have been in the industry since the 1980s and continue to have strong
confidence in it. We now look forward to becoming a shareholder in Lerøy Seafood
Group, which we believe is well positioned for increased value creation in the
future," says Nina Møgster, CEO of Hardhaus and Trollholm.

Using its existing authorisations, Lerøy's Board of Directors may deliver the
consideration shares by issuing new shares and/or by acquiring treasury shares,
including through a combination of the two. The consideration shares received by
Trollholm AS will be subject to an agreed lock-up period following completion.

The agreement contains customary conditions for completion. Completion is
currently expected in early 2027.
