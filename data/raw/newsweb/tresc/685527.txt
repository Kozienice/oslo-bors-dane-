Lerøy Seafood Group ASA will present its results for the third quarter of 2026
on Tuesday, 10 November 2026 at Hotel Continental, Stortingsgata 24/26, Oslo, at
08:00 CET.

Poke bowls will be served from 07:45 CET.

There will be an opportunity to ask questions following the presentation.

The presentation will also be webcast live at www.leroyseafood.com/en/investor/,
where it will be possible to submit questions in writing.

The quarterly report and presentation material will be published at 06:30 CET on
the same day.

A recording of the presentation in English will be available at
www.leroyseafood.com/en/investor/ from 13:00 CET.

This information is subject to the disclosure requirements pursuant to Section
5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
