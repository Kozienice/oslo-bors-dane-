Equinor ASA (OSE: EQNR, NYSE: EQNR) announced on 22 July 2026 a cash dividend
per share of USD 0.39 for second quarter 2026.

The NOK cash dividend per share is based on average USDNOK fixing rate from
Norges Bank in the period plus/minus three business days from record date 16
November 2026, in total seven business days. Average Norges Bank fixing rate for
this period was 9.6021. Total cash dividend for second quarter 2026 is
consequently NOK 3.7448 per share.

On 25 November 2026, the cash dividend will be paid to relevant shareholders on
Oslo Børs (Oslo Stock Exchange) and to holders of American Depositary Receipts
("ADRs") on the New York Stock Exchange.

This information is published in accordance with the requirements of the
Continuing Obligations and is subject to the disclosure requirements pursuant to
section 5-12 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
