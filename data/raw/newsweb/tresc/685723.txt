Veidekke has been commissioned by Tertnes Holding AS to build the Eidsvågbakken
13 office development in Bergen. The project will be carried out as a design and
build contract, valued at approximately NOK 120 million excluding VAT.

The project comprises a new office building with one basement level and four
office floors, totaling approximately 3,300 square metres (GFA). The building
will feature high-quality materials and solutions throughout.

"We look forward to delivering this project in close collaboration with the
client, Tertnes Holding. Together, we have developed an office building with
many attractive qualities, and this type of development allows us to draw on our
extensive experience to create an appealing workplace environment," says Hans
Aakre, District Manager at Veidekke Bygg Bergen.

The project will be delivered through a collaborative contracting model with an
open-book approach.

The basement level will accommodate parking facilities, technical rooms, storage
areas and changing facilities, while floors 1 through 4 will be dedicated to
office space.

The client, Tertnes Holding, will occupy its own premises in the new building in
Åsane.

"We have had a productive collaborative process with Veidekke and look forward
to realising a modern, attractive and future-oriented office building for our
employees and, not least, our tenants here in Eidsvåg, Åsane," says Jan Olav
Jørgensen, Managing Director of Tertnes Holding.

Construction work on site is scheduled to commence in February next year, with
completion planned by the end of November 2028. The contract will be included in
Veidekke's order backlog for the fourth quarter of 2026.

Veidekke (https://www.veidekke.com/) is one of Scandinavia's largest
contractors. In addition to undertaking all types of building and civil
engineering assignments, the group also maintains roads and produces asphalt and
aggregates. Veidekke emphasises stakeholder involvement and local experience.
Its annual turnover is approximately NOK 43 billion, and half of its 8,000
employees own shares in the company. Veidekke is listed on the Oslo Stock
Exchange and has posted a profit every year since its inception in 1936.
