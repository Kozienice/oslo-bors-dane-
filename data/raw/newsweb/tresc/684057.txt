Veidekke present a market update on 21 October at 10:00 CET, presenting its
outlook for the Scandinavian construction markets in 2027 and 2028.

The presentation will cover:

  - market developments and activity levels in Norway, Sweden and Denmark
  - construction cost developments and key drivers expected to influence the
markets in the years ahead

The market update will be webcast live from Veidekke's studio.

As part of the programme, Anne Margrethe Platou, Analyst at DNB Carnegie, will
present her views on climate policy developments and their potential
implications for the construction and civil engineering industry.

The broadcast starts at 10:00 AM. Here is Link to the live broadcast:
Link (https://qcnl.tv/p/-bth0t5HYeAgbzERzhYoqw)

The market update will be conducted in Norwegian.
