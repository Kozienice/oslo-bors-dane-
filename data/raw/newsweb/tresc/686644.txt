Sandnes, Norway, 17 November 2026: The shares in Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) will
be traded ex dividend of NOK 1.368 per share for the third quarter of 2026, as
of today 17 November.

The extraordinary general meeting held on 11 November in Vår Energi resolved to
pay a dividend of NOK 1.368 per share,?relating to Q3 2026. The dividend will be
paid on 25 November in NOK and the NOK dividend amount is based on the daily
exchange rate published by Norges Bank on 20 October at approximately 16:00
CEST.

BlueNord ASA shareholders will receive compensation corresponding to the
dividend of NOK 1.368 per Vår Energi share as cash consideration upon completion
of the combination, in accordance with the approved merger plan. The transaction
is expected to close around year-end.
