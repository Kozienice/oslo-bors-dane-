Please see below information about transactions made under the fourth of the
2026 share buy-back programme for Equinor ASA (OSE:EQNR, NYSE:EQNR, CEUX:EQNRO,
TQEX:EQNRO).

Date on which the buy-back tranche was announced: 28 October 2026.

The duration of the buy-back tranche: 29 October 2026 to no later than 1
February 2027.

Further information on the tranche can be found in the stock market announcement
on its commencement dated 28 October 2026, available here:
https://newsweb.oslobors.no/message/684948

From 29 October to 30 October 2026, Equinor ASA has purchased a total of
265,300 own shares at an average price of NOK 441.0372 per share.

Following completion of the above transactions, Equinor ASA owns a total of
23,861,117 own shares, corresponding to 1.00% of Equinor ASA's share capital,
including shares under Equinor's share savings programme (excluding shares under

shares, corresponding to 0.53% of the share capital).

EU Market Abuse Regulation and that is subject to the disclosure requirements
