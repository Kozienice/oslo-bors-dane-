HENT, a subsidiary of Sentia, has signed a contract with SVG Property for the
construction of a new office building at Jåttå in Stavanger. The contract is
worth NOK 208 million, excluding VAT.

The contract covers an office building of approximately 5,000 square meters
gross floor area (GFA). The agreement follows a project development phase that
HENT and SVG Property carried out in close collaboration.

Construction is scheduled to start in March 2027, with handover expected in
the third quarter of 2028.

-We are pleased with the trust SVG Property has shown in us and look forward
to delivering an attractive office building in an excellent location at Jåttå.
The project is a good example of how close collaboration in the development
phase lays the foundation for successful execution,says Jan Jahren, CEO of
Sentia ASA.
