Sandnes, Norway, 15 October 2026: A total of 126 388 shares were purchased on
behalf of employees in Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) participating in the company's
employee share saving programme. The average purchase price was NOK 54.624 per
share. The transactions are based on savings made in October 2026, and 86% of
Vår Energi employees participated.

As part of the programme, primary insiders in Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) were
allocated 3 982 shares at an average price of NOK 54.624 per share.

Details on individual allocation of shares to primary insiders is included in
the attached notification of trading.
