Chief Financial Officer Hans-Jørgen Wibstad has decided to leave Norwegian Air Shuttle with effect from 31 October 2026 to take up a new role as CFO in Multiconsult ASA on 1 November, and upon completion of the proposed merger, CFO in Multiconsult Rejlers.

"I am leaving Norwegian as a strong and well positioned airline. Over the last few years, the company has delivered solid financial results, distributed a first ever dividend and initiated key strategic growth initiatives. I would like to wish the company and all its great employees all the best for the future," said Hans-Jørgen Wibstad.

Gjermund Aasen, SVP Corporate Finance & Fleet, has been appointed interim Chief Financial Officer with effect from 1 November 2026. He has been with Norwegian since 2021 and was previously Vice President Finance at Golden Ocean Management AS. He holds a Master of Science in Business Administration from Nord University in Bodø and BI Norwegian Business School.

"I would like to thank Hans-Jørgen for his contribution as CFO of Norwegian since 2022. He has played an important role in the company's development over the last years, and I have valued our close collaboration. Both me and the rest of Norwegian team wish him all the best in his future endeavours. I am also very pleased that Gjermund has agreed to step in as interim CFO. Having worked closely with him, I know his expertise will ensure a seamless transition," said Geir Karlsen, CEO of Norwegian.

The company has initiated a search process to appoint a Chief Financial Officer on a permanent basis.
