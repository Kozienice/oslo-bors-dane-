Hydro's adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was NOK 9,089 million, up
from NOK 5,996 million in the same quarter last year. Higher all-in metal prices
and higher alumina prices contributed positively in the quarter, along with
improved earnings in Recycling and Extrusions. The strong performance was
partially offset by higher anode and energy costs in Aluminium Metal, increased
energy costs in Bauxite & Alumina due to gas supply disruptions, a stronger NOK
vs USD, and negative price area differences in Energy.

  * Alunorte resumed regular operations following temporary gas supply
    disruptions

  * Free cash flow of NOK 1.4 billion impacted by higher metal prices and
    associated working capital requirements and prepayments

  * First metal produced at HalZero test facility, marking important milestone
    for carbon-free technology track

  * Commercial momentum continues for Hydro's low-carbon and recycled aluminium
    portfolio

  * New PPAs secured, further strengthening Hydro's long-term power position

Hydro delivered strong profitability in the quarter, with adjusted earnings per
share increasing from NOK 1.0 in the third quarter of 2025, to NOK 2.3 in the
third quarter of 2026. Free cash flow was NOK 1.4 billion, as strong adjusted
EBITDA was partly offset by higher net operating capital, primarily driven by
increased metal prices and associated working capital requirements and
prepayments. While this development reflects the current market environment,
improving cash conversion and working capital efficiency remains a clear
priority going forward. Consequently, Hydro has updated its year-end capex
guiding to around NOK 12.5 billion, down from NOK 13.5 billion, and net working
capital guiding to around NOK 32 billion, up from NOK 30 billion. The twelve
month adjusted RoaCE ended at 12.5 percent.

"Hydro's third quarter results reflect strong operational performance amplified
by market tailwinds. Record high liquid metal production from our Norwegian
smelter system, combined with strong pricing throughout the quarter drove solid
earnings and strong profitability. Operational excellence enables us to capture
more of the upside when markets are strong and, more importantly, builds
resilience when markets turn," says Eivind Kallevik, President and CEO of
Hydro.

During the third quarter, disruption in natural gas supplies to Alunorte
temporarily reduced production at the refinery. Through mitigating actions,
including securing alternative LNG supply, Hydro maintained operational
continuity and successfully restored alumina production.

Aluminium Metal benefited from higher all-in metal prices and strong operational
performance, while Recycling and Extrusions delivered improved earnings
supported by strong recycling margins. Hydro's Norwegian smelters increased
liquid metal production to record levels, reinforcing Hydro's position as a
reliable supplier of low-carbon aluminium to European customers.

In parallel, Hydro experienced continued momentum for its low-carbon and
recycled products, with leading brands like CHANEL Fragrances and Beauty,
General Motors, and Mercedes-Benz integrating Hydro's solutions in their product
lines.

"We continue to see strong demand for our low-carbon and recycled offerings from
some of the world's leading consumer brands. When brands like CHANEL Fragrances
and Beauty and Mercedes-Benz choose Hydro, they place their trust in our
quality, reliability, and ability to help them achieve their ambitions. That
trust has commercial value and reinforces our confidence in the long-term
opportunities for low-carbon and recycled aluminium," says Kallevik.

Hydro reached an important milestone in the development of its HalZero
technology by producing aluminium for the first time, demonstrating the core
electrolysis technology beyond laboratory scale. The achievement marks an
important step toward Hydro's long-term ambition of eliminating emissions from
electrolysis and anode production. Further testing, piloting and technology
development remain before any potential industrial application. Enova awarded
NOK 82 million in funding to support the concept development and planning of an
industrial concept pilot.

"This is an important milestone and a significant step forward in the
development of HalZero. Producing the first metal demonstrates that the
electrolysis technology works and paves the way for the continued development of
a game changing technology with the potential to enable emission-free aluminium
production in the future," says Kallevik.

During the quarter, Slovalco began the restart of primary aluminium production
following the agreement announced in July on long-term framework conditions with
the Slovak government. The restart commenced in late August, marking the return
of capacity curtailed during the European energy crisis in 2022. The first phase
targets an annual production capacity of 75,000 tonnes by the end of February
2027. The restart reflects the importance of competitive power, predictable
regulatory frameworks, and effective industrial policy in maintaining and
rebuilding Europe's industrial base.


Results and market development per business area

Adjusted EBITDA for Bauxite & Alumina decreased compared to the third quarter of
last year, to NOK 283 million from NOK 1,290 million, mainly driven by lower
alumina sales prices, natural gas supply disruptions resulting in higher LNG
sourcing costs and unfavourable BRL/USD exchange rate effects.This was partly
offset by lower raw material costs and lower fixed costs.

PAX traded up throughout the third quarter 2026 to USD 370 per mt at the end on
the quarter, diverging from Chinese alumina prices moving down. Despite lower
alumina production at certain refineries in Indonesia because of raw material
supply challenges, the World ex-China alumina market was oversupplied in the
quarter. China's alumina market became increasingly oversupplied during the
quarter as new refinery capacity came on line.  Chinese alumina prices were
close to the marginal cash production cost. Approximately half of China's
alumina production depends on bauxite imported from Guinea. The government of
Guinea is considering the possibility of restricting annual bauxite exports
volume, but no formal announcement has been made.

Adjusted EBITDA for Energy decreased in the third quarter compared to the same
period last year, to NOK 411 million from NOK 828 million. The decrease is
mainly due to a loss on price area differences compared to a gain in the same
period last year.

Average Nordic power prices increased in the third quarter compared with both
the previous quarter and the same quarter last year. The increase was mainly
driven by weak hydrological conditions and higher power prices in Continental
Europe. Price area differences between the south and north of the Nordic market
were above the previous quarter and below the same period last year. The price
difference between central Norway and northern Sweden widened significantly
compared with both the previous quarter and the same quarter last year.

The Nordic hydrological balance at the end of the quarter was 13 TWh below
normal, compared to 15 TWh below normal at the end of last quarter and 1 TWh
below normal at the same time last year. Norwegian hydropower reservoirs were
around 67.8 percent of full capacity at the end of the quarter, which is below
the normal for this time of year of 82.8 percent. The distribution was uneven,
with lower than normal levels in the southern and central Norway, and higher
than normal levels in the north.

Adjusted EBITDA for Aluminium Metal increased in the third quarter of 2026
compared to the third quarter of 2025, to NOK 7,031 million from NOK 2,732
million, due to higher all-in metal prices, partly offset by lower sales volume,
higher energy and carbon cost, and weaker USD to NOK. Global primary aluminium
consumption was slightly higher compared to the third quarter of 2025, driven by
a 3.0 percent increase in Asia. Primary consumption in the World ex. China is
estimated to be down compared to the third quarter of 2025. The Middle East
crisis continued to affect the aluminium market and prices during the third
quarter. Following indications that the conflict might be nearing an end at the
end of the second quarter, tensions increased again in the third quarter,
directly contributing to higher aluminium prices. Throughout the quarter the
market traded in a range between USD 3,100 per mt and USD 3,300 per mt.
Consequently, the three month aluminium price increased slightly throughout the
third quarter of 2026, starting the quarter at USD 3,076 per mt and ending at
USD 3,170 per mt.

Adjusted EBITDA for Metal Markets decreased in the third quarter of 2026
compared to the same period last year, to NOK 125 million from NOK 154 million,
due to lower results from sourcing and trading activities, partly offset by
higher results from recyclers.

Adjusted EBITDA for Extrusions increased in the third quarter of 2026 compared
to the same quarter last year, to NOK 1,189 million from NOK 1,107 million,
driven by higher recycling margins in combination with increased sales volume,
partly offset by higher maintenance cost, higher SG&A cost and negative currency
effects mainly from stronger NOK/USD.

CRU estimates that European extrusion demand increased by 1 percent in the third
quarter of 2026 compared to the same quarter last year, following weaker market
conditions in the first half of the year. Automotive demand remained positive,
supported by continued growth in battery electric vehicle production. Non-
automotive transportation also turned positive after a weak start to the year,
while building and construction remained broadly stable in the quarter.

CRU estimates that North American extrusion demand remained broadly unchanged in
the third quarter of 2026 compared to the same quarter last year, following a
decline in the first half of the year. Commercial transportation continued to
grow, supported by improving truckload rates, with further growth expected going
forward. The industrial segment also remained solid, supported in part by
continued datacenter investments. Automotive demand remained subdued, reflecting
persistent headwinds in electric vehicle production.


Other key financials

Compared to the second quarter of 2026, Hydro's adjusted EBITDA increased to NOK
9,089 million from NOK 8,923 million, mainly due to higher all-in metal and
alumina prices and lower fixed costs in the third quarter of the year. This was
partially offset by higher energy costs in Bauxite & Alumina due to gas supply
disruptions and seasonally lower Extrusion volumes and margins.

Net income (loss) amounted to NOK 5,263 million in the third quarter of 2026.
Net income (loss) included unrealized derivative losses, mainly on LME related
contracts of NOK 204 million, environmental cost of NOK 111 million,
rationalization charges and closure costs of NOK 81 million, and impairment
charges on fixed assets of NOK 70 million. The tax effect on these adjustments
reflected a standardized tax rate for taxable gains and tax deductible losses.
Adjusted net income (loss) for the first quarter ended at NOK 4,372 million.

Hydro's net debt decreased from NOK 16.3 billion to NOK 15.4 billion during the
third quarter of 2026. The net debt decrease was mainly due to positive EBITDA
contributions, partially offset by net operating capital build, investments and
negative other operating cash flow.

Adjusted net debt decreased from NOK 22.8 billion to NOK 21.9 billion, mainly
driven by the decrease in net debt.

Reported earnings before financial items and tax (EBIT), and net income include
effects that are disclosed in the quarterly report. Adjustments to EBITDA, EBIT,
and net income (loss) are defined and described as part of the alternative
performance measures (APM) section in the quarterly report.
