As announced on October 29, 2026, Kongsberg Gruppen ASA (KONGSBERG) has launched an employee share programme. The subscription period has now closed.

5 216 KONGSBERG employees have subscribed for shares in the annual employee share programme. Total subscriptions amounted to MNOK 368, exceeding the programme's maximum limit of MNOK 220. The total allocation will therefore be reduced to MNOK 220.

As announced on October 29, 2026, KONGSBERG will purchase its own shares for MNOK 220 to meet its obligations under the employee share programme. DNB Carnegie will carry out the share purchases on behalf of KONGSBERG from November 23 to December 20, 2026, as previously announced. The number of shares allocated to each participant will be determined once the purchases have been completed, based on the average purchase price of the shares.

This announcement provides an overview of primary insiders who have subscribed for shares under the programme. The amounts are stated before the 25% discount. Shares acquired under this year's programme are subject to a lock-up period until October 20, 2027.
