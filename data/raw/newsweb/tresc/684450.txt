Sandnes, Norway, 21 October 2026: Reference is made to the announcement by Vår
Energi ASA (OSE: VAR) on 21 October 2026 regarding the notice of an
extraordinary general meeting ("EGM"), to be held on 11 November 2026 to approve
a dividend of NOK 1.368 per outstanding share per 16 November 2026, limited in
aggregate to a maximum of NOK 3 754 884 578 in total, equivalent to
approximately USD 400 million, relating to Q3 2026.

Dividend amount: 1.368

Announced currency: NOK

Date of approval: 11 November 2026

Last day including right: 16 November 2026

Ex-date: 17 November 2026

Record date: 18 November 2026

Payment date: 25 November 2026

The maximum aggregate dividend amount, corresponding to the NOK equivalent of
approximately USD 400 million, assumes that the combination with BlueNord ASA
has been completed by 16 November 2026. Should the combination not be completed
by that date, BlueNord ASA shareholders will instead receive compensation
corresponding to the dividend of NOK 1.368 per Vår Energi share as cash
consideration upon completion of the combination, in accordance with the
approved merger plan.

The dividend will, subject to approval by the EGM, be paid in NOK, and the NOK
dividend amount is based on the daily exchange rate published by Norges Bank 20
October 2026 approximately at 1600 CEST.
