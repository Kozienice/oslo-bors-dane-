Førde, 23rd of November 2026

Fjordavegen Holding AS ("Fjordavegen") has sold 2,029,411 shares in Nordic Mining ASA (the "Company"). Prior to the sale, Fjordavegen owned 6,417,202 shares, equalling 5.13% of the total share capital and voting rights in the Company. Following the sale, Fjordavegen has 4,387,791 shares remaining in the Company, equalling 3.51% of the total share capital and voting rights.

This notification is made public pursuant to section 4-2 of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act.
