Sandnes, Norway, 11 November 2026: The extraordinary general meeting of Vår
Energi ASA (the "Company", OSE: VAR) was held today, 11 November 2026, at 15:00
CET. The meeting was held as a digital event.

In accordance with the proposals made by the Board of Directors, the general
meeting approved a dividend of NOK 1.368 per share, relating to Q3 2026.

Dividend amount: 1.368

Announced currency: NOK

Date of approval: 11 November 2026

Last day including right: 16 November 2026

Ex-date: 17 November 2026

Record date: 18 November 2026

Payment date: 25 November 2026

The dividend will be paid in NOK, and the NOK dividend amount is based on the
daily exchange rate published by Norges Bank on 20 October 2026 at approximately
16:00 CEST.

BlueNord ASA shareholders will receive compensation corresponding to the
dividend of NOK 1.368 per Vår Energi share as cash consideration upon completion
of the combination, in accordance with the approved merger plan. The transaction
is expected to close around year-end.

At the extraordinary general meeting the class B-shareholder elected Paolo
Grossi, Luca Vignati and Claudia Porretta Serapiglia as new class B shareholder
elected Board members, with effect from today's meeting, replacing Francesco
Gattei, Francesca Rinaldi and Claudia Almadori, respectively. Marco Bollini and
Claudia Vignati were elected by the class B-shareholder as deputy Board members
for the class B shareholder elected Board members.

All resolutions were approved in accordance with the proposals set out in the
notice for the extraordinary general meeting dated 21 October 2026. The complete
minutes from the meeting will be made accessible on
https://varenergi.no/en/about-us/organisational-structure-and-governance/general
-meeting/.
