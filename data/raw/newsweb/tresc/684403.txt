(Arendal, Norway / Sherbrooke, QC, Canada - 20 October 2026)

Tekna (OSE: TEKNA), a world-leading provider of advanced materials and plasma systems, is pleased to announce that the company's Materials business area has received significant orders totalling CAD 1.64 million for titanium metal powder. The orders are expected to be delivered throughout 2027. 

The orders were placed by an established distributor serving medical device manufacturers globally. They are for Ti64 titanium alloy powder in the particle size range for laser powder bed fusion (LPBF), an additive manufacturing process used to build complex, high-precision parts. 

"As one of the fastest growing markets for additive manufacturing, the medical sector continues to see strong demand for patient specific devices and implants requiring high-quality materials. These orders reflect the strength of our long-standing relationship with this distributor and support our continued efforts to expand our presence in the medical sector," said Tekna CEO Claude Jean.

Suppliers to the medical device industry are typically required to hold certified quality management systems. Tekna has an established track record supplying the medical sector, and its Materials business area recently renewed its ISO 13485:2016 certification for the design and manufacture of medical devices. The certification confirms that Tekna's processes and materials meet the strict requirements set for medical applications.

The new orders will be included in the Company's order intake for the fourth quarter of 2026.

Tekna will publish its interim report for the third quarter on November 5, 2026.

ABOUT TEKNA HOLDING ASA
Tekna is a world-leading provider of sustainable, advanced material solutions, headquartered in Sherbrooke, Canada. The company specializes in high-purity metal powders used in critical applications such as additive manufacturing (3D printing) across the aerospace, defense, medical and consumer electronics industries. The company is positioning itself in the fast-growing market of advanced nanomaterials for the microelectronics sector. 

Tekna also develops cutting-edge induction plasma systems designed for both industrial research and production. Its unique, IP-protected plasma technology is powering its hypersonic wind tunnels, PlasmaSonic, which enable simulating material exposure conditions in space. 

With over 30 years of experience, Tekna is a trusted partner to a broad portfolio of multinational blue-chip customers for its high-quality products and innovation. Its material solutions help enhance productivity, enable more efficient use of materials and support the transition to more resilient supply chains and a circular economy.
