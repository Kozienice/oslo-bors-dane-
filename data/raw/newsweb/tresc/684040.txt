Aker BP (OSE: AKRBP) will publish its report for the third quarter of 2026 on 29
October 2026. Today, the company provides preliminary production and sales
figures for the quarter.

Production and sales
Aker BP's equity production for the quarter averaged 374.3 thousand barrels of
oil equivalent per day (mboepd). Due to overlift, the net volume sold in the
quarter was 395.7 mboepd.

Volume (mboepd)            Q3-26  Q2-26
Net production             374.3  383.6
Overlift/(underlift)        21.4  (8.0)
Net volume sold            395.7  375.7
Of which liquids           343.3  322.1
Of which natural gas        52.4   53.5

Realised prices (USD/boe)  Q3-26  Q2-26
Liquids                    100.6  107.9
Natural gas                123.3   88.4

About Aker BP
Aker BP is a company engaged in exploration, field development and production of
oil and gas on the Norwegian continental shelf. The company operates the field
centres Alvheim, Eiga, Skarv, Ula and Valhall, operates Yggdrasil, which is
under development, and is a partner in the Johan Sverdrup field. Measured in
production, Aker BP is one of the largest independent oil companies in Europe.
Aker BP is headquartered at Fornebu and is listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange
under the ticker code AKRBP. More about Aker BP at
www.akerbp.com (https://www.akerbp.com/en).
