(Arendal, Norway / Sherbrooke, QC, Canada - 3 November 2026)

Tekna (OSE: TEKNA), a world-leading provider of advanced materials and plasma systems, is pleased to announce that the company has received significant orders for titanium metal powder amounting to CAD 1.2 million. The orders are expected to be delivered throughout 2027. 

The orders were placed by a major US-based contract manufacturer of medical implants. They comprise Ti64 titanium alloy powder in the particle size range suited to laser powder bed fusion (LPBF), an additive manufacturing process used to build complex, high-precision components. 

"We are pleased to receive these orders, confirming the strong demand for our powders in medical applications," said Tekna CEO Claude Jean. "The growing use of advanced implants and wider adoption of additive manufacturing technologies across the healthcare sector are driving rapid growth in the market for advanced materials. The orders we have received this quarter further strengthen our position as a trusted materials supplier to medical device manufacturers." 

Suppliers to the medical device industry are typically required to hold certified quality management systems. Tekna has a well-established track record in the medical sector, and its Materials business area has recently renewed its ISO 13485:2016 certification for the design and manufacture of medical devices. The certification confirms that Tekna's processes, materials and quality system meet the stringent requirements of medical applications. 

Tekna will publish its interim report for the third quarter on November 5, 2026.

ABOUT TEKNA HOLDING ASA
Tekna is a world-leading provider of sustainable, advanced material solutions, headquartered in Sherbrooke, Canada. The company specializes in high-purity metal powders used in critical applications such as additive manufacturing (3D printing) across the aerospace, defense, medical and consumer electronics industries. The company is positioning itself in the fast-growing market of advanced nanomaterials for the microelectronics sector. 

Tekna also develops cutting-edge induction plasma systems designed for both industrial research and production. Its unique, IP-protected plasma technology is powering its hypersonic wind tunnels, PlasmaSonic, which enable simulating material exposure conditions in space. 

With over 30 years of experience, Tekna is a trusted partner to a broad portfolio of multinational blue-chip customers for its high-quality products and innovation. Its material solutions help enhance productivity, enable more efficient use of materials and support the transition to more resilient supply chains and a circular economy.
