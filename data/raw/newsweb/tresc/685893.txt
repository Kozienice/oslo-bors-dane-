HENT, a subsidiary of Sentia, has today signed a contract with the Norwegian
Broadcasting Corporation (NRK) for phase 2 of NRK's new headquarters at
Normannsløkka, Ensjø, in Oslo. The contract value is approximately NOK 2.800
million, excluding VAT.

The contract is a design-and-build contract based on collaboration (samspill).
HENT and NRK have worked together in phase 1 since April 2025 which is now
completed. The project now moves into the construction phase, with completion
planned for Desember 2029.

The building will have a floor area of 53500 square metres and will include
studios, production facilities, offices and public areas. The site is located
close to railway and metro lines, which places particular demands on the
execution of the project. The project is a candidate to become a FutureBuilt
pilot project, a programme for climate-friendly buildings with high
architectural quality. This involves stricter requirements for reduced
greenhouse gas emissions than for conventional buildings.

-Over the past 18 months, we have developed the project through open
collaboration with NRK and our partners. This provides a solid foundation for
delivering a technically demanding project. NRK is one of Norway's most
important public institutions, and the new headquarters will be a building
open to the public and a boost for Ensjø and eastern Oslo. It is a building we
are proud to construct, says Jan Jahren, CEO of Sentia.
