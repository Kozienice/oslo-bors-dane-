JPMorgan Chase & Co. ("JPMC ") has a notifiable interest in the share capital of BlueNord ASA as a result of trades by its subsidiary J.P. Morgan Securities PLC. The total number of shares held by JPMC on 05 November was 1,760,104 being 6.884% of the issued share capital and voting rights of the company. The holding in JPMC crossed a notification threshold of 5% on 05 November 2026.
