Aker BP employees have subscribed for approximately 675,000 shares under the
company's annual employee share programme.

The subscription price was NOK 282.10 per share, representing a 20 percent
discount to the reference market price of NOK 352.62 per share. The shares are
subject to a three-year lock-up period.

Approximately 50 percent of the company's employees participated in the
programme, including the following primary insiders and close associates:

About Aker BP
Aker BP is a company engaged in exploration, field development and production of
oil and gas on the Norwegian continental shelf. The company operates the field
centres Alvheim, Eiga, Skarv, Ula and Valhall, operates Yggdrasil, which is
under development, and is a partner in the Johan Sverdrup field. Measured in
production, Aker BP is one of the largest independent oil companies in Europe.
Aker BP is headquartered at Fornebu and is listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange
under the ticker code AKRBP. More about Aker BP at
www.akerbp.com (https://www.akerbp.com/en).

This information is subject to disclosure requirements pursuant to section 5-12
of the Norwegian Securities Trading Act and requirements under the EU Market
Abuse Regulation.
