Strøm Gundersen, a company in AF Gruppen, has entered into an agreement with Drammen Helsepark AS for the construction of the BK2 Spinneriet project. The contract is a design-and-build contract valued at NOK 673 million, excluding VAT.

The agreement covers the construction of BK2 Spinneriet, a nine-story building with approximately 14,290 square meters of gross floor area (GFA), together with a 3,180-square-meter underground parking facility that will extend the existing parking garage at Drammen Helsepark. Upon completion, BK2 Spinneriet will house a municipal emergency care center, an emergency medical communications center (AMK), and a healthcare facility providing short-term patient accommodation. The building will achieve a BREEAM Excellent environmental certification.

"We are very pleased to have been selected to deliver BK2 Spinneriet. The project is well aligned with our expertise and experience from complex construction projects in the Drammen region. We look forward to continuing our strong collaboration with Drammen Helsepark and contributing to the further development of Brakerøya," says Tormod Solberg, EVP Building at AF Gruppen.

Construction is scheduled to commence in May 2027, with completion and handover planned for March 2030.
