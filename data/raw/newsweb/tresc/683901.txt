Sandnes, Norway, 12 October 2026: Vår Energi ASA (OSE: VAR, the "Company") will
publish its financial report for the third quarter 2026 on Wednesday 21 October.
Today, the Company provides an update on production, sales volumes and other
relevant items.

Vår Energi's net production of oil, liquids and natural gas averaged 369 kboepd
in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 2% from the second quarter and flat
compared to the third quarter last year. The Company expects full-year
production of around 390 kboepd, compared to the original guidance of 390-410
kboepd, reflecting operational issues at Johan Castberg and the Jotun FPSO in
July and production irregularities in the Åsgard area. Johan Castberg and Jotun
FPSO have been producing at expected levels since early-August. None of the main
production impacts are reservoir related and have been managed effectively.

The production split in the first quarter was 68% oil and NGLs (liquids) and 32%
gas. Total volumes produced were 34.0 mmboe whereas volumes sold in the quarter
amounted to 34.8 mmboe. The overlift position is mainly due to timing of crude
liftings in the quarter. Vår Energi obtained an average realised price (volume
-weighted) of USD 103 per boe in the quarter. The realised crude price was USD
103 per barrel, representing a premium of USD 6 per barrel compared to dated
Brent. The increase in premium differentials to Brent observed since early
September will be reflected in fourth quarter realised prices, as cargoes are
normally sold weeks ahead of delivery. The realised gas price of USD 108 per boe
is approximately 18 USD below the average spot market reference price. Volumes
sold on gas year ahead and fixed price contracts represented 29% of third
quarter gas volumes sold at an average price of USD 87 per boe. 25% of third
quarter volumes were sold with month ahead indexation in a rising market
(approximately USD 22 per boe below spot), whereas the impact will be the
opposite in a falling market.

Other items

As previously communicated, free cash flow in the third quarter was impacted by
two cash tax payments totaling approximately NOK 10.8 billion (USD 1.1 billion),
approximately USD 100 million related to the closing of the Pandion acquisition
and the DNO swap, and a USD 350 million dividend payment for the second quarter,
paid in September.

The information above is based on a preliminary assessment of the Company's
third quarter 2026 financial results and may be subject to change until the
financial statements have been finally approved and published by the Company.
