NRC Group ASA is pleased to invite investors, analysts, media and other
stakeholders to its Capital Markets Update and third quarter 2026 presentation
on Thursday 5 November.
 
The company will provide information on NRC Group's operations, markets,
financial development and outlook. The Capital Markets Day presentation
material and the third quarter 2026 results will be published the same day at
07.00 AM (CEST).
 
The event will be held in House of Oslo, Ruseløkkveien 34, Oslo, and will
start at 09.00 AM (CEST) and is expected to last approximately 2.5 hours,
including Q&A. The presentations will be given in Swedish/Norwegian.
 
Topics that will be covered in CMU:
 
  About NRC Group
  Status on first half of strategy period
  Market drivers and outlook second half of strategy period
  Financial performance and priorities 
 
Speakers at the event:
 
  Anders Gustafsson - CEO
  Åsgeir Nord - CFO
