23 November, 2026 - Aker Solutions has received a letter of award for a
substantial1 contract with an undisclosed client in the oil and gas sector.

Aker Solutions expects that a binding contract will be signed during Q4 2026 and
will subsequently book the award as order intake.

1Aker Solutions defines a 'substantial' contract as one being valued between NOK
2.5 billion and NOK 4 billion.
