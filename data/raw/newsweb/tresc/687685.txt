AF Gruppen ASA (the "Company") has decided to initiate a share buyback program. The buyback program will be conducted in accordance with the authorization provided by the annual General Meeting to the Board of Directors on May 13, 2026.

The share buyback program will be for a maximum of 200,000 shares and will comprise acquisitions of own shares for an aggregate amount of up to NOK 42,000,000. The program will commence on November 30 and will end no later than December 14, 2026. Buyback transactions will be executed according to the market price on the Oslo Stock Exchange. The shares purchased under this program will be used in connection with the Company's employee share and incentive programs.
