Nedre Romerike vann- og avløpsselskap IKS has appointed NRC Norge AS, a
company wholly owned by NRC Group ASA,  to contract for ground, civil and
water and wastewater works in Sørumsand.
 
The contract is valued at approximately NOK 38 million, and the work will
commence in November 2026 and is scheduled for completion in October 2027.

47 957 34 985.
