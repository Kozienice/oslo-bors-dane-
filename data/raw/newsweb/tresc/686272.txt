Veidekke achieved revenue of NOK 11.8 billion in the third quarter, and a pre
-tax profit of NOK 904 million. The quarterly order intake totalled NOK 8.3
billion and brought the group's order book to NOK 52.6 billion at quarter-end.

"Veidekke has had a strong third quarter, with both revenue and profit up year
-on-year. Activity levels are high, capacity utilisation is good and margins are
robust across the project portfolio. The profit improvement in the quarter was
particularly strong in Norway, in both the construction and infrastructure
operations," says Group CEO Jimmy Bengtsson.

"Thanks to a robust order intake of NOK 8.3 billion in the quarter, the order
book remains at a high level. Orders due to be executed in the next 12 months
are 14 percent higher than a year ago, providing a solid starting point for
future activity," says Bengtsson.

Veidekke's quarterly revenue totalled NOK 11.8 billion, up 11 percent from NOK
10.7 billion in Q3 2025. Activity levels were up across all of Veidekke's
operations in the third quarter.

The third-quarter pre-tax profit amounted to NOK 904 million, up from NOK 721
million in Q3 2025. The profit improvement was mainly driven by increased
activity levels and better capacity utilisation, particularly in the Norwegian
operations. The PTP margin improved to 7.7 percent, up from 6.8 percent in Q3
2025.

The group's quarterly order intake was NOK 8.3 billion, compared to NOK 7.2
billion in the same period last year. At quarter-end, the order book amounted to
NOK 52.6 billion, compared to NOK 47.5 billion at the same time last year. Some
59 percent of the order book will be converted into revenue in the next 12
months.

The group's LTI rate (lost-time injuries per million hours worked) was 3.7 in
the third quarter, on par with the preceding quarter and down from 5.0 in Q3
2025. No serious injuries occurred during the quarter. The quarterly sick leave
rate was 4.2 percent, down from 4.7 percent in the preceding quarter and from
the same quarter last year.

The net interest-bearing position totalled NOK 4.9 billion as at 30 September
2026, compared to NOK 2.7 billion one year ago. Cash flow from operational
activities in the first three quarters of the year amounted to NOK 3.4 billion,
up from NOK 1.8 billion in the corresponding period in 2025. Total assets were
NOK 21.4 billion as at 30 September, up from NOK 19.0 billion at the same time
last year. The increase in total assets is primarily attributable to increased
activity levels.

Veidekke is one of Scandinavia's largest contractors. In addition to undertaking
all types of building and civil engineering assignments, the group also
maintains roads and produces asphalt and aggregates. Veidekke emphasises
stakeholder involvement and local experience. Its annual revenue is
approximately NOK 43 billion, and half of its 8,000 employees own shares in the
company. Veidekke is listed on the Oslo Stock Exchange, and has posted a profit
every year since its inception in 1936.
