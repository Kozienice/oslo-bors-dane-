Reference is made to the stock exchange notice 25 June 2026 regarding the NGO
Neptune Network STI's filing for injunction claiming that Engebø Rutile and
Garnet AS shall halt its mining operations.

The district court has as expected today ruled in favor of Engebø Rutile and
Garnet AS concluding that the conditions for an injunction are not met.

The operations at the mine continues as planned.
