Subsea 7 S.A. Treasury Shares
Luxembourg – 2 October 2026 – Subsea 7 S.A. (Oslo Børs: SUBC, ADR: SUBCY) (the “Company”)
announced that the Company has transferred 848,961 shares held in treasury to satisfy employee share
awards under the Company’s Long-term Incentive Plan.
Following the above transfer, the number of common shares held in treasury is 2,603,275 representing
0.87% of the Company's issued common shares. The total number of shares in issue, including treasury
shares, is unchanged at 299,600,000.
