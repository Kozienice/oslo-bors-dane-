Market development Platts PAX development
The Platts alumina index (PAX) averaged USD 348 per mt in the third quarter
2026, compared to USD 308 per mt in the second quarter 2026. PAX was 2
percent lower compared to the third quarter 2025 (USD 357 per mt).
PAX traded up throughout the third quarter 2026 to USD 370 per mt at the end
on the quarter, diverging from Chinese alumina prices moving down. Despite
lower alumina production at certain refineries in Indonesia because of raw
material supply challenges, the World ex-China alumina market was
oversupplied in the quarter. China's alumina market became increasingly
oversupplied during the quarter as new refinery capacity came on line.
Chinese alumina prices were close to the marginal cash production cost.
Approximately half of China’s alumina production depends on bauxite
imported from Guinea. The government of Guinea is considering the
possibility of restricting annual bauxite exports volume, but no formal
announcement has been made.
In the period from July and August 2026, China imported 716kt of alumina
mainly from Australia and Indonesia, a 225 percent increase compared with
the same period last year (220kt). Alumina exports from China to Russia
continued, reaching 406kt in the period compared to 356kt in the
corresponding period last year. China was a net importer of alumina in July
and August 2026 (310kt) compared to net exports of 135kt in the
corresponding period a year ago.
In the period from July to August 2026, China imported 34 million mt of
bauxite, 10 percent lower than the corresponding period a year ago. Imports
from Guinea decreased 7 percent whereas imports from Australia decreased 3
percent compared to the same period last year; those two countries
accounted for 98 percent of total imports in July and August 2026. The
average Chinese bauxite import price was USD 65 per mt CIF in the period
under review, 10 percent below the corresponding period last year (USD 72
per mt).

Market development Energy spot price
Average Nordic power prices increased in the third quarter compared with
both the previous quarter and the same quarter last year. The increase was
mainly driven by weak hydrological conditions and higher power prices in
Continental Europe. Price area differences between the south and north of the
Nordic market were above the previous quarter and below the same period
last year. The price difference between central Norway and northern Sweden
widened significantly compared with both the previous quarter and the same
quarter last year.
The Nordic hydrological balance at the end of the quarter was 13 TWh below
normal, compared to 15 TWh below normal at the end of last quarter and 1
TWh below normal at the same time last year. Norwegian hydropower
reservoirs were around 67.8 percent of full capacity at the end of the quarter,
which is below the normal for this time of year of 82.8 percent. The distribution
was uneven, with lower than normal levels in the southern and central Norway,
and higher than normal levels in the north.

Market development Premiums
The Middle East crisis continued to affect the aluminium market and prices
during the third quarter. Following indications that the conflict might be nearing
an end at the end of the second quarter, tensions increased again in the third
quarter, directly contributing to higher aluminium prices. Throughout the
quarter the market traded in a range between USD 3,100 per mt and USD
3,300 per mt. Consequently, the three month aluminium price increased
slightly throughout the third quarter of 2026, starting the quarter at USD 3,076
per mt and ending at USD 3,170 per mt.
European duty paid standard ingot premiums ended the third quarter at USD
555 per mt, compared to USD 558 per mt at the end of the second quarter.
The US Midwest premium decreased from USD 2,396 per mt at the beginning
of the quarter to USD 2,374 per mt at the end of the quarter.
Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE) prices increased by USD 247 per mt
from start of the quarter to the end, ending at USD 3,560 per mt. Average for
the quarter was up USD 75 per mt compared to the second quarter.
Global primary aluminium consumption was slightly higher compared to the
third quarter of 2025, driven by a 3.0 percent increase in Asia. Primary
consumption in the World ex. China is estimated to be down compared to the
third quarter of 2025.
For 2026 external sources are estimating a global deficit of primary aluminium
at around 0.7 million mt, which could be reduced in case of a quicker ramp-up
of Middle Eastern production in addition to increased production in the rest of
the world.
The European demand for extrusion ingot and sheet ingot increased slightly in
the third quarter 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. Consumption of
primary foundry alloys was stable in the third quarter 2026 compared to the
year before.
Total global stocks at the end of the third quarter of 2026 were estimated to be
8.9 million mt, down 0.5 million mt compared to the third quarter 2025 and
down 0.6 million mt compared to the second quarter 2026.

Additional factors impacting Hydro
The accumulated LME hedge in Hydro as of September 30, 2026 amounted to
115 thousand tonnes for the remainder of 2026, 430 thousand tonnes for 2027
and 50 thousand tonnes for 2028. This has been achieved using both
commodity derivatives and currency derivatives. Parts of the raw material
exposure is also hedged, using both fixed price physical contracts and
financial derivatives.
The total USD/BRL hedge in place at Alunorte and Albras amounts to
approximately USD 89 million for the remainder of 2026 and USD 380 million
for 2027.
In August 2026, Alunorte was informed by its supplier CELBA of disruptions to
natural gas availability. Consequently, Alunorte has not received contracted
gas deliveries and is sourcing gas in the market at spot-based prices, while
progressing a long-term gas supply solution on competitive terms. The third
quarter 2026 financial impact for Bauxite & Alumina from reduced production
and gas purchased above the contract price is approximately USD 85 million.
The gas arbitrage impact for Alunorte in the fourth quarter of 2026 is estimated
at USD 90 - 110 million. The financial impact remains uncertain and is subject
to gas prices, contractual developments and the implementation of a long-
term solution.
